# Voice and Style Specifications

Ensure all narrative drafts follow these constraints:

- **Narrative Authenticity Directive**: `_config/narrative_authenticity.md` is the governing document for making prose and story structure read as human. Its lexical anti-slop list supersedes the short list below; its structural rules bind Stage 02 planning. Word-level fixes alone are NOT sufficient — structural tells (linear plots, explained themes, no subplots) survive style editing and must be planned out, not edited out.
- **Anti-Slop Directive (quick reference)**: Avoid weak filler verb structures ("began to", "started to", "seemed to") and repetitive AI words ("delve", "testament", "tapestry"). Full banned/rationed list in the authenticity directive.
- **Camera Viscosity Dials** (vary per scene — uniform High viscosity is itself an AI tell):
  - `[High Viscosity]` — Relentless close-up sensory detail (sight, sound, touch, smell, taste).
  - `[Medium Viscosity]` — Balanced scene progression and subtextual dialogue.
  - `[Low Viscosity]` — Summarized transitions and wide camera pacing.
