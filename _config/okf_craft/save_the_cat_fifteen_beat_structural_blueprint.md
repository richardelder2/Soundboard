# Save the Cat! 15-Beat Structural Blueprint

Translates classical three-act dramaturgy into a mathematically disciplined 15-beat blueprint tied to manuscript percentages.

---

## 1. Master 15-Beat Pacing Spreadsheet

```
ACT I (0–20%)          ACT II (20–80%)                                ACT III (80–100%)
[01][02][03][04][05]   [06][07][08] === [09] === [10][11][12]         [13][14][15]
Set-Up & Catalyst      Fun & Games   Midpoint   Bad Guys Close In     Break 3 & Finale
```

| # | Beat Name | Target % | Story Function & Reader Contract |
|---|---|---|---|
| **01** | **Opening Image** | 0%–1% | Snapshot of flawed "before" world; establishes starting baseline flaw. |
| **02** | **Theme Stated** | 5% | Secondary character voices the core truth/Need (dismissed by hero). |
| **03** | **Set-Up** | 1%–10% | Ordinary life, stasis, the Lie lived by, and A-story supporting cast. |
| **04** | **Catalyst** | 10% | Life-altering external disruption that cannot be ignored. |
| **05** | **Debate** | 10%–20% | Hero hesitates: *"Can I do this? What happens if I refuse?"* |
| **06** | **Break into Two** | 20% | Active, irreversible choice to leave old world for the unfamiliar. |
| **07** | **B-Story** | 22% | Introduction of the thematic relationship embodying the Need. |
| **08** | **Fun & Games** | 20%–50% | **Promise of the Premise:** trailer scenes exploring powers/world. |
| **09** | **Midpoint** | 50% | False Victory or False Defeat; stakes become real; clock begins. |
| **10** | **Bad Guys Close In** | 50%–75% | External pressure mounts; internal ally fractures surface. |
| **11** | **All Is Lost** | 75% | Lowest point; apparent goal destruction; "Whiff of Death". |
| **12** | **Dark Night of Soul**| 75%–80% | Mourning the old life and failed Lie; moment before epiphany. |
| **13** | **Break into Three** | 80% | Epiphany combining A-story goal with B-story truth. |
| **14** | **Finale** | 80%–99% | 5-Point Finale execution securing transformation. |
| **15** | **Final Image** | 100% | Visual mirror of Opening Image proving complete change. |

---

## 2. The 5-Point Finale Architecture (Beat 14)

1. **Gathering Team:** Rallying allies with Truth discovered at Break into Three.
2. **Executing Plan:** Storming the stronghold; initial tactics succeed.
3. **High Tower Surprise:** Board flipped; antagonist anticipated the maneuver.
4. **Digging Deep Down:** Stripped of tricks/tools; irrevocable moral sacrifice made.
5. **Executing New Truth:** Transformed self acts purely from courage and vulnerability.
