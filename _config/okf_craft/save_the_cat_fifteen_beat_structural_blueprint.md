# Save the Cat! The 15-Beat Structural Blueprint & Pacing Percentages

The *Save the Cat!* structural framework translates classical three-act dramaturgy into a **rigorous, mathematically disciplined 15-beat blueprint**. 

By binding major emotional shifts to precise manuscript percentage targets, it guarantees that narrative propulsion, mystery setup, character regression, and climactic synthesis occur at mathematically optimal intervals for maximum reader engagement.

---

## 1. Master 15-Beat Pacing Spreadsheet

```
ACT I (0% - 20%)              ACT II (20% - 80%)                                ACT III (80% - 100%)
[01][02][03][04][05]  |===>   [06][07][08] === [09] === [10][11][12]   |===>   [13][14][15]
Set-Up & Catalyst             Fun & Games   Midpoint   Bad Guys Close In       Break 3 & Finale
```

| # | Beat Name | Target % | Story Function & Reader Contract |
|---|---|---|---|
| **01** | **Opening Image** | 0% – 1% | Snapshot of protagonist's flawed "before" world; establishes starting tone and baseline flaw. |
| **02** | **Theme Stated** | 5% | A secondary character makes a statement to the protagonist stating the core truth of the book (which the hero dismisses). |
| **03** | **Set-Up** | 1% – 10% | Establishes the hero's ordinary life, the status-quo stasis, the Lie they live by, and introduces the A-story supporting cast. |
| **04** | **Catalyst (Inciting Incident)** | 10% | Life-altering external disruption that shatters the ordinary world. Cannot be ignored or undone. |
| **05** | **Debate** | 10% – 20% | The hero hesitates, resists, or seeks an easy escape. Asks: *"Can I really do this? What happens if I refuse?"* |
| **06** | **Break into Two** | 20% | The protagonist makes an active, irreversible choice to leave the old world and step into the unfamiliar realm. |
| **07** | **B-Story** | 22% | Introduction of the thematic relationship (love interest, mentor, rival) who embodies the spiritual lesson/Need. |
| **08** | **Fun & Games** | 20% – 50% | **The Promise of the Premise.** The trailer moments of the book; protagonist explores new powers/world. |
| **09** | **Midpoint** | 50% | **The Great Shift.** Either a *False Victory* (hero thinks they won, but stakes soar) or *False Defeat*. A ticking clock is introduced; stakes become real. |
| **10** | **Bad Guys Close In** | 50% – 75% | External pressure intensifies from the antagonist while internal fractures, secrets, and doubts tear the hero's allies apart. |
| **11** | **All Is Lost** | 75% | The lowest emotional point. The apparent destruction of the original goal. Accompanied by a literal or symbolic "Whiff of Death". |
| **12** | **Dark Night of the Soul** | 75% – 80% | The hero mourns the loss of their old life and the failure of their Lie. The moment right before the epiphany. |
| **13** | **Break into Three** | 80% | The "Aha!" epiphany. The hero combines the A-story goal with the B-story thematic truth to forge a radical new plan. |
| **14** | **Finale** | 80% – 99% | The 5-Point Finale execution: Gathering the team $ightarrow$ Executing the new plan $ightarrow$ The High Tower Surprise $ightarrow$ Digging deep $ightarrow$ Executing the Truth. |
| **15** | **Final Image** | 100% | The visual mirror of the Opening Image, proving beyond doubt that the character and their world have fundamentally transformed. |

## 2. The 5-Point Finale Architecture (Beat 14 Deep Dive)

The climax of Act III must never be a single lucky punch or a prolonged fistfight. It requires five sequential movements:

1. **Gathering the Team:** The hero rallies remaining allies, armed with the new Truth discovered at Break into Three.
2. **Executing the Plan:** The team storms the villain's stronghold or initiates the complex solution; initial beats succeed.
3. **The High Tower Surprise:** The plan fails! The antagonist anticipated it or a catastrophic complication flips the board.
4. **Digging Deep Down:** Deprived of all tricks, weapons, or mentors, the protagonist must look inside and make an irrevocable moral sacrifice.
5. **Executing the New Truth:** The protagonist acts purely from their transformed self, securing victory through the synthesis of courage and vulnerability.
