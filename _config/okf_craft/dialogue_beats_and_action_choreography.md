# Dialogue Choreography & Rhythm

Dialogue is not just a transcript of talking heads; it is a physical, timed performance where physical action beats replace dialogue tags and silence speaks louder than words.

---

## 1. The Anti-Tag Directive

*   **Banning Adverbial Tags:** Strictly ban tags like `said angrily`, `replied nervously`, `whispered quietly`.
*   **Replacing Tags with Action Beats:** Replace `he said` with physical behavior that reveals emotion and establishes space:
    *   *Weak:* "I don't think we should go," Mark said nervously.
    *   *Strong:* Mark checked the bolt on the side door twice, his thumb catching on the brass. "I don't think we should go."
*   **Two-Speaker Flow:** When two characters are in an established exchange, drop all tags and beats for 3–5 lines to let the rhythmic velocity build, then interrupt with a physical beat.

---

## 2. Micro-Timing: Pauses, Hesitations & Interruptions

*   **The Em-Dash Cut (Interruption):** Use an em-dash (`—`) without spaces when a speaker is abruptly cut off by another character or sudden action:
    *   "We can still make the—" The windshield exploded.
*   **The Physical Pause:** Never write "there was a pause." Render what happens during the silence (the kettle whistling down, coffee grounds dripping into the glass pot).
