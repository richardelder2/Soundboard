# Possible Worlds Theory & Modal Discrepancy Matrix

Marie-Laure Ryan’s Possible-Worlds Theory models stories as multi-dimensional modal systems. Around the Textual Actual World (TAW) orbits Alternative Possible Worlds (APWs) projected by character minds.

---

## 1. Five Core Modal Worlds

1. **Knowledge Worlds ($K$-Worlds):** Epistemic beliefs regarding what is true in TAW. Divergence creates dramatic irony and tragic error.
2. **Wish Worlds ($W$-Worlds):** Axiological preferences and desires. Distance from TAW fuels motivation.
3. **Obligation Worlds ($O$-Worlds):** Deontic network of moral laws, taboos, and duties. Clash with $W$-worlds creates agonizing guilt.
4. **Intention Worlds ($I$-Worlds):** Operational action plans to transform TAW toward $W$-world desire.
5. **Fantasy Universes ($F$-Universes):** Oneiric dreams, hallucinations, and counterfactual fictions.

---

## 2. Modal Discrepancy Matrix

```
[TAW: Objective Reality] <--- Collision ---> [K-World: Flawed Beliefs]
           ▲                                             ▲
           │                                             │
[W-World: Raw Desire]    <--- Collision ---> [O-World: Moral Duty]
```

### Major Modal Conflicts
* **Tragic Blindspot ($K \neq \text{TAW}$):** Rational action based on false belief (*Oedipus*, *Othello*).
* **Moral Crucible ($W \perp O$):** Desired object requires violating ethical code or law (*Lancelot*).
* **Tactical Impasse ($I \not\rightarrow \text{TAW}$):** Plan collapses when reality resists assumptions.
* **Double Bind ($O_1 \perp O_2$):** Inviolable duties demand mutually exclusive actions.

---

## 3. Governing Principles

* **Minimal Departure:** Readers assume the world matches physical/psychological reality except where explicitly altered. Focus energy on departure points.
* **Diversification:** Narrativity is a function of modal distance between APWs and TAW. Perfect alignment kills tension.

---

## 4. Scene-Planning Diagnostic

* [ ] **What do they mistakenly believe?** ($K$-world error)
* [ ] **What do they crave secretly?** ($W$-world desire)
* [ ] **What rule forbids it?** ($O$-world boundary)
* [ ] **What physical action bridges the gap?** ($I$-world execution)
