# Experientiality: The Embodied Human Consciousness Primitive

Monika Fludernik's revolutionary breakthrough in *Natural Narratology* proved that narrative does **not** begin with plot or events. Narrative begins with **Experientiality**: the quasi-mimetic evocation of real-life human consciousness, cognitive processing, and emotional response to an event.

Without an experiencing consciousness, a list of events is merely a chronology or a weather report, not a story.

---

## 1. The Core Equation of Experientiality

```
[EXTERNAL EVENT]  +  [COGNITIVE INTERPRETATION]  +  [SOMATIC/EMBODIED AFTERMATH] = NARRATIVE
```

*   **The Sterile Sequence (0% Experientiality):** "The bomb detonated at 14:00. Three buildings collapsed."
*   **The Experiential Reality (100% Narrative):** "The concussion hit my teeth before my ears registered the sound—a dry, alkaline vacuum that sucked the air out of my throat while the plaster dust rained down like warm snow."

---

## 2. Invariant Rules for SAGA Drafting & Auditing

1. **The Sensorimotor Grounding Rule:**
   * Every major plot event must be perceived through an embodied sensorimotor filter (skin temperature, acoustic frequency, respiratory hitch, muscular tension).
2. **The Cognitive Reaction Gap:**
   * The narrator does not merely register facts; they register the *meaning* of the fact against their existing emotional trauma, biases, and active desires.
