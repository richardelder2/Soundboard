# Chapter Endings & Momentum Continuity

A great chapter ending compels the reader forward not through cheap tricks, but by **shifting dramatic potential energy**. Relying solely on physical cliffhangers creates reader fatigue; master novelists rotate through **nine distinct ending energies**.

---

## 1. The Nine Ending Energies

1. **Unanswered Question:** A character asks for truth or opens an envelope; cut before disclosure.
2. **Changed Plan:** The old strategy burns; character commits to an outrageous new tactic.
3. **Epiphanic Reversal:** A new clue permanently inverts the meaning of prior events.
4. **Threatened Consequence:** An external clock or deadline is stamped onto the world.
5. **False Resolution:** Apparent triumph achieved, undercut by looming disaster.
6. **Emotional Rupture:** An alliance or relationship is fractured by an unforgivable word.
7. **Symbolic Image Echo:** A haunting visual motif crystallizes the cost of the ordeal.
8. **Deliberate Quiet:** Exhausted, contemplative stillness after trauma, before the next climb.
9. **Physical Peril:** Genuine mortal danger paused at the turning point.

---

## 2. Eliminating Cheap Teasers

* **Banned False Hooks:** Never end with *"Little did she know..."* or *"Someone was watching"* without concrete story pressure.
* **Overshot Exit:** Stopping 300 words after the true climax while characters tidy dishes. Cut on the impact beat.
* **Momentum Bridging:** Next chapter's opening question should shift angle or tempo, not replay the same beat.

---

## 3. Multi-Path Revision Options

* **Option A (Clean Truncate):** Cut trailing summary; exit cleanly on the crisp turning line.
* **Option B (Looming Choice):** End when the character realizes they have only two bad options.
* **Option C (Atmospheric Freeze):** Close on a sensory image holding the chapter's thematic weight.
