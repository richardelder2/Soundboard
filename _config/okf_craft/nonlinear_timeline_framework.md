# Nonlinear Timelines: The Architecture of Anachrony

Chronological order is the default of life, but narrative interest often relies on anachrony: reordering events to control dramatic tension, thematic resonance, and the pacing of revelation.

---

## Strategic Modes of Nonlinearity

1. **Analepsis (Flashbacks / The Past Invading):**
   * Do not drop flashbacks merely for background biography. A past scene should only be rendered when an event in the present *forces* the memory to the surface as an emotional obstacle or clue.
2. **Prolepsis (Flash-Forwards / Inevitability Anchors):**
   * Teasing a future catastrophe creates anticipatory dread. The reader reads to discover *how* it happened, not *what* happens.
3. **The Re-Reading Revelation:**
   * Design at least one nonlinear reveal that completely re-contextualizes earlier interactions when the truth is revealed.
4. **Thematic Mirroring:**
   * Juxtapose two timelines (e.g., past vs. present) so that the turning points of both occur simultaneously in adjacent chapters.
