# Courtly Intrigue & Political Machinations

Political fiction and courtly intrigue do not depend on physical violence. Tension is sustained through **Information Warfare**, public theater, debt leverage, and proxy assassinations.

---

## 1. The Triad of Courtly Conflict

1. **The Mask vs. The Whisper:** Characters must display absolute smiling deference in open court while simultaneously planting poisoned rumors or economic sanctions behind tapestries.
2. **Factional Cannibalism:** There are never just two opposing sides (e.g., King vs. Rebels). Every faction is a vipers' nest of internal rivalries where allies will betray each other the moment a seat opens.
3. **Debt as Lethality (The Ledger Weapon):** Power is measured by who holds whose secrets, mortgages, and family scandals.

---

## 2. Invariant Rules for Intrigue Beats

*   **The Public Humiliation Duel:** High-stakes battles fought entirely through etiquette, protocol seated arrangements, and witty verbal barbs where showing anger is an admission of defeat.
*   **The Unavoidable Complicity:** To survive in court, the protagonist must compromise an innocent party to protect their own position.
