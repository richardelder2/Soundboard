# Mythic Character Masks & Dynamic Staging

Archetypes are dramatic functions and psychological masks, not rigid character labels. A single character can dynamically shift masks across a story's progression.

---

## 1. Eight Primary Mythic Masks

* **1. The Hero (Ego in Search of Identity):** Audience surrogate and vehicle of empathy. Types: Willing/Active, Reluctant, or Catalyst (transforms others).
* **2. The Mentor (Higher Self / Conscience):** Provides training, gifts, and moral correction. Variations: Fallen/Corrupt, Dark, Comic Wise Fool.
* **3. The Threshold Guardian (Test of Worthiness):** Blocks passage at thresholds until the hero displays growth. Manifests internal doubt; bypassed by cunning or alliance over force.
* **4. The Herald (Call to Disruption):** Issues the Call to Adventure, signaling necessity of change (person, message, event).
* **5. The Shapeshifter (Prism of Doubt):** Loyalty and motives fluctuate; drives romantic tension, paranoia, and projection of the Anima/Animus.
* **6. The Shadow (Opposing Will / Inverse):** Antagonistic force presenting greatest danger. Best Shadows are heroes of their own journey believing their ruthlessness justified.
* **7. The Ally (Humanist Anchor / Foil):** Accompanies hero, testing assumptions and externalizing interior monologue into dialogue.
* **8. The Trickster (Disruptor of Pretension):** Punctures inflated egos with comic relief and necessary chaos; reminds ego of fallibility.

---

## 2. Dynamic Mask-Shifting

```
[Scene 1: Mentor Mask] ---> [Scene 5: Threshold Guardian] ---> [Scene 12: Shapeshifter]
```

* *Best Friend:* Reliable **Ally** in Act I $\rightarrow$ blocks reckless choice as **Threshold Guardian** $\rightarrow$ pulls subversive stunt in climax as **Trickster**.
* *Love Interest:* Elusive **Shapeshifter** $\rightarrow$ delivers crucial news as **Herald** $\rightarrow$ offers healing truth as **Mentor**.

---

## 3. Staging Rules

1. **Preserve Core Identity:** The dramatic mask shifts, but core wound and worldview remain continuous.
2. **Serve Hero Growth:** Masks challenge unintegrated aspects of the protagonist's psyche.
3. **Rotate Weight:** Focus each scene on one dominant dialectic tension rather than crowding all masks.
