# Story Grid Core Macro Architecture

The Story Grid framework ensures every unit of fiction (scene, sequence, act, global arc) represents a human value shift from positive to negative or vice versa.

## Five Primal Scene Movements
1. **Inciting Incident:** An event (causal or coincidental) upsets the life balance of the protagonist.
2. **Progressive Complications:** Obstacles escalate the stakes, culminating in a crisis.
3. **Crisis:** A dilemma presenting a choice between two irreconcilable goods or the lesser of two evils.
4. **Climax:** The active decision made by the character under pressure.
5. **Resolution:** The resulting new normal and value shift.
