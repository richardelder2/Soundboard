# Possible Worlds Theory: The Virtual Narrative Sphere

Marie-Laure Ryan established that a story does not consist merely of what *actually* happens in the physical world. A rich narrative is a dynamic solar system composed of the **Textual Actual World (TAW)** surrounded by a halo of **Virtual Private Worlds** projected by characters.

---

## 1. The Virtual Private Worlds

Dramatic conflict erupts when there is a mismatch between what physically exists in the world and what characters construct in their minds:

1. **Wish Worlds (W-Worlds):** What characters desire or dream will happen.
2. **Obligation Worlds (O-Worlds):** What characters believe they *must* do based on moral duty, law, or taboos.
3. **Knowledge Worlds (K-Worlds):** What characters believe to be true (which may be totally mistaken or manipulated).
4. **Foresight Worlds (F-Worlds):** What characters predict will happen based on current trajectories.

---

## 2. Invariant Law of Plot Complexity

*   **Flat Fiction:** Only tracks the physical actual world (`Event A happens in reality, then Event B happens in reality`).
*   **Deep Narrative Fiction:** Tracks the collision between worlds:
    *   *Tragedy:* When the character's **Obligation World** (duty) destroys their **Wish World** (love).
    *   *Irony:* When the character's **Knowledge World** is fatally out of sync with the **Textual Actual World** (they believe the poison is medicine).
    *   *Suspense:* When the reader sees the **Actual World** preparing to demolish the character's naive **Foresight World**.
