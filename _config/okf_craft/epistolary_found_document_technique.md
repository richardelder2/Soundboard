# Epistolary & Found-Document Architecture

Telling a story through fragmented documents (letters, audio logs, incident reports, chat transcripts) transforms the reader from a passive spectator into an active forensic investigator.

---

## 1. Core Mechanics of Document Fiction

*   **Polyphonic Voice Variety:** Each document must reflect the distinct vocabulary, cadence, and social standing of its creator (e.g., bureaucratic jargon vs. panicked diary scrawl).
*   **The Power of Redaction & Omission:** The most crucial story turns often live in what is missing—a redacted timestamp, an intercepted email with missing attachments, or bloodstained paper.
*   **Physicality of the Medium:** Mention physical traits of the artifact (ink bleed, coffee stains, timestamp metadata corruptions).

---

## 2. Cross-Genre Hybridization

*   **Sci-Fi Horror (The Derelict Vessel):** The story of an abandoned ship told through fragmented system diagnostics, automated maintenance logs, and desperate crew voice memos.
*   **Political Thriller:** A leak investigation rendered entirely through declassified memos and encrypted message logs.
