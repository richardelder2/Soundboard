# Enneagram Character Engine & Disintegration Trajectories

The Enneagram maps internal conflict between Want and Need through the Core Wound, Central Lie, and Disintegration Trajectory under escalating pressure.

---

## 1. Three Centers of Conflict

1. **Gut / Instinct (Types 8, 9, 1):** *Anger & Control.* Preserving physical autonomy against violation.
2. **Heart / Feeling (Types 2, 3, 4):** *Shame & Identity.* Deserving love, admiration, and authentic significance.
3. **Head / Thinking (Types 5, 6, 7):** *Fear & Security.* Navigating danger and unpredictability in the world.

---

## 2. Nine Archetypal Fixations in Fiction

| Type | Fixation | Core Fear | Central Lie | Armor / Symptom |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1: Reformer** | Perfectionist | Corrupt / flawed | "Flawless or unworthy." | Rigid; suppressed simmering rage. |
| **2: Helper** | Rescuer | Unwanted / disposable | "Loved only when needed." | Smothering devotion; guilt bids. |
| **3: Achiever** | Performer | Worthless failure | "I am only my trophies." | Chameleonic deceit; emotional numbing. |
| **4: Individualist**| Tragic Outcast| Insignificant | "Doomed to be misunderstood." | Melancholic withdrawal; aestheticized pain. |
| **5: Investigator** | Detached Scholar| Helpless / depleted | "Hoard facts to stay untouched."| Cold detachment; intellectualized trauma. |
| **6: Loyalist** | Sentinel | Betrayed / insecure | "Danger is everywhere." | Hyper-vigilance; testing allies. |
| **7: Enthusiast** | Hedonist | Trapped in pain | "Facing grief will destroy me."| Thrill-seeking escapism; avoids depth. |
| **8: Challenger** | Titan / Tyrant | Controlled / weak | "Softness invites destruction." | Belligerence; pre-emptive domination. |
| **9: Peacemaker** | Mediator | Conflict / separation | "Safer to remain invisible." | Passive aggression; numbing withdrawal. |

---

## 3. Disintegration & Integration Trajectories

```
[Type Armor] ---> (Pressure Escalation) ---> [Stress Line: Disintegration]
                                                      │
                                           (Epiphany / Sacrifice)
                                                      ▼
                                            [Growth Line: Integration]
```

* **Type 8:** Stress $\rightarrow$ 5 (brooding isolation, paranoia); Growth $\rightarrow$ 2 (nurturing protection).
* **Type 3:** Stress $\rightarrow$ 9 (burnout, apathetic dissociation); Growth $\rightarrow$ 6 (loyalty to higher cause).
* **Type 1:** Stress $\rightarrow$ 4 (bitter self-pity, alienation); Growth $\rightarrow$ 7 (joyful grace, humor).

---

## 4. Crafting the Arc

1. **Backstory Trauma:** The origin event establishing the Central Lie as survival armor.
2. **Coping Armor:** The conscious persona deployed in Act I.
3. **Climactic Dilemma:** Climax where external success requires surrendering the Lie to embrace the integration line.
