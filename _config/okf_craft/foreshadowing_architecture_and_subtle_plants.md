# Foreshadowing Architecture: Engineering Inevitability

The highest achievement of a narrative climax is to be **unpredictable yet totally inevitable**. Foreshadowing is the craft of planting the seeds of that inevitability without prematurely alerting the reader.

---

## 1. The Triad of Foreshadowing Modes

1. **Atmospheric / Meteorological Cues:**
   * Weather, light quality, and environmental decay reflecting an impending crisis long before characters perceive it.
2. **The Throwaway Dialogue Irony:**
   * A character makes a casual, joking remark in Chapter 2 that turns out to be literal and tragic in Chapter 24.
3. **Symbolic Micro-Events (The Fractal Precursor):**
   * A small, self-contained interaction in Act 1 that mirrors the exact emotional geometry of the Act 3 climax (e.g., a child deciding whether to mercy-kill an injured bird foreshadows the protagonist pulling the plug on their mentor).

---

## 2. Invariant Rules for SAGA

*   **The Retroactive Clarity Gate:** A reader reaching the climax should be able to flip back to the foreshadowing scenes and realize the clues were in plain sight.
*   **Never Announce Foreshadowing:** Banning narrator intrusions like "little did he know that this was the last time he would see her." Let the events stand on their own.
