# Dwight Swain's MRU Architecture & The Pacing Velocity Equation

Scene momentum alternates between **Scenes** (action phases) and **Sequels** (processing phases), governed by granular **Motivation-Reaction Units (MRUs)**. Pacing fails when reflective sequels dilate during high-stakes crises.

---

## 1. The Macro-Rhythm: Scene vs. Sequel

### The Scene (Proactive Drive)
1. **Goal:** Immediate, concrete objective sought right now.
2. **Conflict:** Physical or social obstacles raised by environment or opponent.
3. **Disaster:** Complication denying the goal ("No, and furthermore...") or granting it with a devastating catch ("Yes, but...").

### The Sequel (Reactive Processing)
1. **Reaction:** Visceral and emotional response to the disaster.
2. **Dilemma:** Evaluation of dwindling options under risk.
3. **Decision:** Commitment to a new urgent goal, launching the next Scene.

---

## 2. Motivation-Reaction Units (MRUs)

Human perception processes external stimuli in an unalterable neuro-physiological sequence:

```
[Stimulus / Motivation] ---> [1. Visceral Reflex] ---> [2. Cognitive Appraisal] ---> [3. Action / Speech]
```

### Invariable Order:
1. **Motivation (External):** Objective sensory input (*"The deadbolt clicked."*).
2. **Reaction 1 (Visceral):** Involuntary nervous response (*"Her breath snagged in her throat."*).
3. **Reaction 2 (Cognitive):** Psychological appraisal (*"He wasn't supposed to be home until dawn."*).
4. **Reaction 3 (Action/Speech):** Voluntary movement or utterance (*"She slipped the knife into her sleeve and stepped behind the door."*).

*Rule:* Never invert this sequence (e.g. scream before seeing the threat); inversion violates reader cognitive reality.

---

## 3. The Pacing Velocity Equation

$$\text{Pacing Velocity} = \frac{\text{Words of Proactive Action (Scene)}}{\text{Words of Internal Processing (Sequel)}}$$

### Compression Trajectory:

* **Act I (0%–25%):**
  * *Ratio:* ~60% Scene : 40% Sequel.
  * *Function:* Establishes internal wound, baseline world, and status quo (2,500–3,500w chapters).
* **Act II (25%–75%):**
  * *Ratio:* ~75% Scene : 25% Sequel.
  * *Function:* Accelerates pressure; sequels compress directly into emergency decisions (2,000–3,000w chapters).
* **Act III (75%–100%):**
  * *Ratio:* ~90% Scene : 10% Sequel.
  * *Function:* Deliberation is stripped; protagonist acts on instinct and immediate existential choice (1,000–1,800w chapters).
