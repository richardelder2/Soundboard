# Brandon Sanderson's Laws of Magic System Engineering

Speculative magic and technology are dramatic problem-solving mechanisms operating under reader contracts to avoid cheap *deus ex machina*.

---

## 1. Zeroth Law: Primacy of Wonder
> *"Always err on the side of what is awesome."*
The concept must ignite genuine aesthetic wonder before constraints are applied; once introduced, bound it by the Three Laws.

---

## 2. First Law: Understanding & Problem-Solving
> *"Satisfying problem-solving with magic is proportional to how well the reader understands said magic."*

```
[SOFT MAGIC: Mystery & Awe] <=================> [HARD MAGIC: Logic & Agency]
- Gandalf, Lovecraft, Miyazaki                  - Allomancy (Mistborn), Sympathy
- Reader ignorant of rules/limits.               - Reader understands rules, costs, limits.
- CREATES problems & wonder.                    - SOLVES problems through character agency.
- Never solves climax (feels cheap).            - Resolves climax by exploiting known rules.
```

---

## 3. Second Law: Limitations Over Powers
> *"Limitations are greater than powers."*
What a user **cannot do** or the **price paid** generates tension:
1. **Fuel / Ingestion Cost:** Consumed resource (metals, lifespans, sanity).
2. **Hard Boundaries:** Absolute negatives (cannot raise dead, cannot create gold).
3. **Backlash / Somatic Cost:** Physical/mental degradation (burns, corruption).
4. **Vulnerability Window:** Conditions neutralizing user (water, severed earth contact).
5. **Sensory Signature:** Alerting manifestations (ozone smell, acoustic hum).

---

## 4. Third Law: Extrapolation Cascade
> *"Expand what you already have before you add something new."*
Extrapolate consequences of 1–2 premises across culture:
* **Warfare:** Levitation replaces vertical castle walls with sunken bunkers and overhead nets.
* **Law & Crime:** Truth magic forces decoy ciphers or suicide pills for assassins.
* **Economy:** Monopoly over fuel sources drives global currency and class warfare.
* **Domestic:** Everyday use for lighting hearths or preserving food.

---

## 5. Magic Design Matrix

| Parameter | Diagnostic Question | Red Flag (AI Tell) |
|---|---|---|
| **Source & Fuel** | What fuels the power? | Infinite free energy. |
| **Clear Costs** | What is lost with exertion? | Free use without bodily/moral toll. |
| **Strict Limits** | What 3 things can it never do? | Solves whatever scene demands. |
| **Societal Impact**| How did society adapt? | Setting unchanged by magic's existence. |
| **Climax Payoff** | Won by clever combination of known rules?| Brand-new spell in final chapter. |
