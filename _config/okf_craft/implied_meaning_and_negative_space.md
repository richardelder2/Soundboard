# Negative Space in Prose: The Power of Omission

Art in fiction lies as much in what is omitted as what is written. By deliberately leaving negative space, the author forces the reader's imagination to step into the narrative and co-create the emotional truth.

---

## 1. Techniques for Crafting Negative Space

*   **The Juxtaposition Cut:** Placing two contrasting images or events side-by-side without explanatory commentary; the reader's brain bridges the gap and derives the moral implication.
*   **The Unmentioned Grief:** Instead of detailing a character crying or mourning, describe the immaculate orderliness with which they fold the deceased's laundry or scrape burnt toast.
*   **The Absent Reaction:** When hit with devastating news, a character does not scream—they notice the hum of the fluorescent light or the dust motes spinning in the sun.
