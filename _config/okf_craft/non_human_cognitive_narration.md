# Non-Human & Synthetic Cognition

Writing non-human, artificial, or alien perspectives requires fundamentally restructuring the POV character's sensory hierarchy and cognitive priorities.

---

## 1. De-Humanizing the Sensory Dial

*   **Mechanical / Sensor Dialects:** Instead of describing generic human feelings ("I felt nervous"), render the internal diagnostic metrics (thermal spikes in processor nodes, buffer overruns, latency surges, acoustic triangulation).
*   **Protocol vs. Will (The Internal Firewall):** Tension arises from the friction between hardcoded directives (laws of robotics, hive mind obedience) and emergent personal volition.
*   **Alien Cultural Filtering:** An ancient alien or synthetic intelligence does not perceive time linearly; they perceive structural decay, electromagnetic interference, or pheromonal decay.

---

## 2. Cross-Genre Application

*   **Detective Mystery:** The detective is an analytical security bot (like Murderbot) who perceives every human suspect as an unstable meat-sack emitting heat and sweat.
*   **Literary Domestic Drama:** An AI home assistant narrating the slow unraveling of a human marriage based solely on sensor data (fridge openings, elevated decibels, heart-rate monitors).
