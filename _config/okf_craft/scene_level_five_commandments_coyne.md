# The Five Commandments of the Micro-Scene

The scene is the fundamental quantum of storytelling. Shawn Coyne’s Story Grid establishes that every functional scene must execute five mandatory structural commandments to avoid dead weight on the page.

---

## 1. The Five Atomic Commandments

```
[1. Inciting Incident] ---> [2. Progressive Complications]
                                       │
                                       ▼
                               [3. Crisis Question] (Binary Dilemma)
                                       │
                                       ▼
[5. Resolution & Shift] <--- [4. Climax Decision]
```

### 1. The Inciting Incident
* **Function:** Upsets local equilibrium, giving the POV character an immediate goal.
* **Types:**
  * *Causal:* Intentional act by an agent (arrest warrant served, ally refuses order).
  * *Coincidental:* Environmental shift or accident (engine dies, blizzard traps caravan).

### 2. Progressive Complications
* **Function:** Obstacles and active pushback from setting, systems, or rivals thwarting initial plans.
* **Escalation Law:** Each obstacle forces greater energy expenditure, vulnerability, or stakes.

### 3. The Crisis Question (Core Engine)
* **Function:** Easy options vanish. Character faces a strict binary choice:
  * *Form A: Best Bad Choice:* Both paths entail severe loss/sacrifice (reveal secret identity to save an innocent OR preserve cover and watch them hang).
  * *Form B: Irreconcilable Goods:* Two righteous outcomes cannot co-exist; choosing one permanently forfeits the other.

### 4. The Climax (Irrevocable Action)
* **Function:** Active execution of the choice in physical movement or spoken dialogue.
* **Rule:** Must be externalized and irrevocable, altering reality—not passive reflection.

### 5. The Resolution (New Equilibrium & Value Shift)
* **Function:** Immediate aftermath of the climactic decision.
* **Value Shift:** Ends with a tangible life-value transition (e.g., *Trust $\rightarrow$ Suspicion*; *Safety $\rightarrow$ Peril*; *Ignorance $\rightarrow$ Revelation*).

---

## 2. The Scene Audit Checklist

1. **Inciting Incident:** What disrupted the status quo in the opening beats?
2. **Progressive Complications:** Did the character face at least two escalating obstacles?
3. **Crisis Question:** Strict binary framed as *"Choose X at cost A, or Y at cost B?"*
4. **Climax:** What irrevocable physical action or statement occurred?
5. **Value Shift:** Name the polarity shift (+/-).
