# Anthropological Worldbuilding

Rather than treating worldbuilding as a list of magical spells or weapon statistics, anthropological worldbuilding grounds speculative worlds in societal behavior, taboos, rituals, and material culture.

---

## 1. The Cultural Iceberg

*   **Material Culture (Surface):** Foodways, dress, architecture, tools, hygiene practices.
*   **Social Organization (Mid-depth):** Kinship systems, labor distribution, legal sanctions, class mobility.
*   **Cosmology & Deep Axioms (Foundation):** What does this society consider sacred? What is unthinkable? How do they conceptualize death, time, and debt?

---

## 2. Worldbuilding Delivery Rules in Prose

1. **The Incurious Insider Rule:**
   * A native character does not marvel at their own technology or explain daily rituals to themselves. Deliver world facts exclusively through interaction and mundane friction.
2. **Conflict Between Axioms:**
   * Generate plot tension not from abstract "evil" but from irreconcilable cultural values colliding (e.g., individualist honor vs. collective survival).
3. **Taboo as Plot Engine:**
   * Every compelling fictional society has severe taboos. Forcing the protagonist to violate a sacred taboo to achieve a personal want creates organic dramatic stakes.
