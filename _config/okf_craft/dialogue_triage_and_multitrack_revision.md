# Dialogue Diagnostic Triage & Multi-Track Revision

When dialogue feels lifeless, writers and agents often resort to shallow fixes: adding quirky catchphrases, exaggerated phonetic accents, or ornate dialogue tags (*"he retorted haughtily"*). Authentic dialogue revision works through **syntactic rhythm, subtextual friction, and physical choreography**.

---

## 1. Common Dialogue Failure Modes

| Symptom | Underlying Craft Defect | Surface Error |
|---|---|---|
| **Talking Heads** | Characters exist in a spatial void; zero physical resistance. | Alternating quoted lines with no sensory grounding. |
| **Ping-Pong Civility** | Perfect turn-taking; polite responses; zero interruption. | AI consensus humming; characters answering every query. |
| **Interchangeable Voices** | Uniform sentence lengths, identical grammar, equal wit. | Stripping tags makes speaker identity unrecognizable. |
| **Thesaurus Tags** | Author mistrusts dialogue, forcing emotional verbs into tags. | *"she grumbled"*, *"he spat"*, *"they opined"*. |

---

## 2. Tag & Action Beat Discipline

```
[Standard Carrier]   --> "said", "asked" (Invisible, non-intrusive punctuation).
[Physical Action Beat]--> Grounded movement replacing tag: "Elena set the wrench down. 'It won't hold.'"
[Banned Ornate Tags] --> "gasped", "smirked", "growled", "huffed", "intoned", "snapped".
```

* **The Attribution Rule:** Use `said` or `asked` for rhythm; use physical action beats to anchor blocking and pacing. Never use a dialogue tag to describe physical speech mechanics (*"he smiled"* is not a vocal verb).
* **Tag Stripping:** In rapid 2-character exchanges, drop tags entirely for 3–5 beats once speaker polarity is clear.

---

## 3. The 5-Track Revision Playbook

When an agent diagnoses weak dialogue, present the author with these explicit creative paths:

* **Track 1 (The Cut Pass):** Strike 30% of lines. Eliminate hellos, goodbyes, throat-clearing pleasantries, and explicit agreement.
* **Track 2 (Physical Choreography):** Anchor the exchange in an ongoing physical task (cooking, reloading, examining ledgers) that mirrors or contrasts the subtext.
* **Track 3 (Heighten Status Asymmetry):** Force one character to speak in clipped monosyllables while the other uses aggressive polite verbosity.
* **Track 4 (Misdirection / Talk Past):** Have Speaker B ignore Speaker A's question to pursue their own hidden priority.
* **Track 5 (Preserve Style Intentionally):** Confirm whether stylized or archaic dialogue (courtly banter, hardboiled clipped staccato) is an intended aesthetic choice.
