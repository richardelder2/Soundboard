# Psychic Distance & Narrative Zoom

Psychic Distance measures perceived intimacy between reader, narrator voice, and character sensory reality. Masterful prose avoids uncontrolled whiplash by treating distance as a fluid cinematic lens.

---

## 1. John Gardner’s Five Levels

```
[Level 1: Panoramic]       <-- Aerial Drone
        │
[Level 2: Objective Exterior] <-- Street Camera
        │
[Level 3: Observable Action]  <-- Eye-Level Tracking Shot
        │
[Level 4: Direct Perception]  <-- Over-the-Shoulder / Close-Up
        │
[Level 5: Visceral Interior]  <-- Neural Stream
```

* **Level 1: Panoramic (Maximum Distance):** Detached, historical scale.
  * *Ex:* *"It was the winter of 1853, and cholera had followed the snow melt into Vienna."*
* **Level 2: Objective Exterior (Medium-Wide):** Figures in physical setting.
  * *Ex:* *"A tall man in a salt-stained greatcoat turned from the wharf into the alley."*
* **Level 3: Observable Behavior (Eye-Level Medium):** Specific actions without internal commentary.
  * *Ex:* *"He pulled his collar up, shook water from his brim, and checked the silver watch."*
* **Level 4: Direct Perception (Close-Up / Character Filter):** Sensory input filtered through immediate cognition.
  * *Ex:* *"The glass was misted. Five past midnight. The carriage was late, or the driver had sold the address."*
* **Level 5: Visceral Stream (Extreme Close-Up / Pure Interiority):** Unmediated stream; tags vanish.
  * *Ex:* *"Damn her. She took the notes. She’s already across the river."*

---

## 2. Graded Stepping

1. **Zooming In (Establishing $\rightarrow$ Intimacy):** $1 \rightarrow 2 \rightarrow 3 \rightarrow 4 \rightarrow 5$. Grounds physical space before interior stakes.
2. **Zooming Out (Intimacy $\rightarrow$ Cosmic):** $5 \rightarrow 4 \rightarrow 3 \rightarrow 2 \rightarrow 1$. Reveals indifferent scale after trauma or climax.
3. **Psychic Whiplash Error:** Leaping directly from Level 1 to Level 5 without intermediate steps shatters immersion.

---

## 3. Diagnostic Checklist

* [ ] **Camera placement conscious?** Intentional Level 1 wide setup vs. Level 4 sensory entry.
* [ ] **Transitions stepped?** Moving between exposition and panic traverses observable physical action (Level 3).
* [ ] **Attributions eliminated at Level 5?** Purge *"he felt"*, *"she realized"*, *"he thought"*.
