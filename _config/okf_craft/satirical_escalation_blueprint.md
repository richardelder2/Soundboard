# Satirical Escalation & Institutional Irony

Satire uses exaggerated institutional logic to reveal real-world absurdities. The power of satire comes from treating ludicrous premises with deadpan bureaucratic seriousness.

---

## 1. The Satirical Engine

*   **The Exaggerated Axiom:** Take a genuine societal flaw (e.g., hyper-bureaucracy, algorithmic addiction, corporate greenwashing) and push it to its extreme logical conclusion.
*   **The Straight Face Rule:** Characters must never wink at the camera or act like they know their world is ridiculous. They must treat absurd regulations as matters of life, death, and solemn civic duty.
*   **The Catch-22 Loop:** Rules designed so that obeying them guarantees failure, while disobeying them is prohibited.

---

## 2. Cross-Genre Application (The Playground)

*   **Satirical Sci-Fi / Space Opera:** An alien war fought purely to satisfy quarterly corporate earnings reports.
*   **Satirical Romance:** A courtship governed entirely by credit scores and algorithmic risk-assessment contracts.
