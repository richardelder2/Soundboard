# Character Arc Anatomy: Lie, Wound, Want & Need

Character transformation balances an internal dialectic: an ingrained defensive delusion (**The Lie**) versus objective moral reality (**The Truth**).

---

## 1. Four Pillars of the Transformation Engine

```
[GHOST / WOUND] ---> Adopts Defensive Armor ---> [THE LIE]
  (Past Trauma)                                      │
                                                     ▼
[THE NEED]      <--- Challenged by Reality <--- [THE WANT]
(Spiritual Truth)    (Ordeal & Climax)          (External Goal)
```

1. **Ghost / Wound:** Historical origin event where vulnerability brought betrayal, shame, or catastrophe. Justifies defense mechanism.
2. **The Lie:** Distorted survival belief ("Never love anyone to never get hurt"; "Power guarantees safety"). Protective in Act I, a prison in Act II.
3. **The Want (External Goal):** Measurable objective pursued across Acts I–II. Protagonist believes achieving it heals pain while keeping the Lie.
4. **The Need (Internal Truth):** Unacknowledged truth required for wholeness ("Vulnerability is the price of connection"). Surrenders the Lie.

---

## 2. Arc Progression Across the Three Acts

| Stage / Beat | Movement | State | Behavioral Symptom |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Act I (0%–25%)** | *Normal World* | **Clinging to Lie** | Defends status quo; uses flaw as shield. |
| **Act II-A (25%–50%)** | *New World* | **Lie Under Strain** | Pursues Want with old habits; cracks show. |
| **Midpoint (50%)** | *Mirror Moment* | **Glance at Truth** | Glimpses self without Lie; shifts proactive. |
| **Act II-B (50%–75%)** | *Rising Friction* | **Want vs. Need War** | Lie fails; embracing Truth is terrifying. |
| **All Is Lost (75%)** | *Dark Night* | **Death of Lie** | Want fails; Lie shatters; bottom hit. |
| **Climax (80%–95%)** | *Crucible* | **Truth in Action** | Sacrifices Want to embody Need; secures victory. |
| **Resolution (95%–100%)**| *Equilibrium* | **Individuated Self** | Lives Truth; transformed worldview mirrored. |

---

## 3. Three Major Arc Typologies

1. **Positive Change Arc:** Believes Lie $\rightarrow$ Overcomes Lie $\rightarrow$ Embraces Truth $\rightarrow$ Transforms self and world.
2. **Flat Arc:** Knows Truth $\rightarrow$ Enters corrupt world $\rightarrow$ Endures tests without breaking $\rightarrow$ Transmutes environment (*Sherlock*, *Katniss*).
3. **Tragic / Corruption Arc:** Choice between Truth and Lie $\rightarrow$ Embraces darker Lie $\rightarrow$ Wins Want at total moral cost (*Michael Corleone*, *Walter White*).
