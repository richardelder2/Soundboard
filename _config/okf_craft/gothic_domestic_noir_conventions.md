# Gothic & Domestic Noir: Conventions & Reader Expectations

Gothic fiction and modern Domestic Noir share identical psychological architecture: terror is not out in the wilderness—**terror is inside the home, the marriage, or the family ancestry**.

---

## 1. Obligatory Reader Contracts

1. **The Sentient Setting (The House as Co-Conspirator):**
   * The mansion, estate, or modern high-rise is not mere architecture; it is an active antagonist holding physical memories, hidden chambers, and oppressive acoustic resonance.
2. **The Gaslight Engine (Epistemic Self-Doubt):**
   * The protagonist is manipulated into doubting their own sanity, memory, or senses. Small items move, conversations are denied, and reality is systematically destabilized.
3. **The Family Sins / Generational Rot:**
   * The crisis is rooted in an unpunished crime, shame, or betrayal committed by ancestors or spouses decades prior.
4. **The Trap of Domesticity:**
   * Social expectations (being a good wife, an obedient daughter, a gracious hostess) prevent the protagonist from screaming or running, turning etiquette into an executioner.

---

## 2. Cross-Genre Hybridization

*   **Sci-Fi Gothic:** A generation ship whose ancestral AI behaves like a jealous Victorian matriarch; the hydroponics bay is a haunted graveyard of early colonists.
*   **Cozy Gothic:** An inherited bed & breakfast where the ghost is polite, but the historical diary reveals a chilling town conspiracy.
