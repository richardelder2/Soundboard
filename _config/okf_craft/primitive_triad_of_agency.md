# The Triad of Agency: The Engine of Empathy

Humans do not bond with characters because they are "likable" or "virtuous." Humans bond with characters who display **Agency under Pressure**.

Agency is mathematically defined by three irreducible elements:

```
    [CONCRETE WANT]  ──────>  [UNYIELDING OBSTACLE]  ──────>  [IRREVOCABLE CRISIS CHOICE]
```

---

## 1. The Three Primitives of Agency

1. **The Urgent, Concrete Want:**
   * Not a vague desire ("wants to be happy"). It must be an actionable, physical goal ("wants to steal the key from the captain's belt before dawn").
2. **The Active Obstacle:**
   * A force equal to or greater than the character's desire (another character's opposing want, physical physics, societal law).
3. **The Irrevocable Crisis Choice:**
   * When the obstacle blocks the initial plan, the character is forced into a crisis choice.
   * **The Invariant Choice Test:** The dilemma must be either:
     * *A choice between two irreconcilable goods* (saving the child vs. saving the city).
     * *A choice between the lesser of two evils* (amputating the leg vs. dying of gangrene).
   * **Cost / Irreversibility:** The choice must carry a permanent consequence. If the choice can be easily undone in the next scene, agency was an illusion.
