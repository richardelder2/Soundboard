# Historical Fiction & Period Immersion

Authentic historical fiction is not about reciting costume dates and royal family trees. It is about rendering the **Historical Double Horizon**: the terrifying reality that past humans held completely different moral axioms than we do today.

---

## 1. The Trap of Presentism

*   **Banning Modern Anachronistic Ethics:** Never make your 14th-century peasant talk like a 21st-century human rights activist. Authenticity demands showing how deeply people believed in divine kingship, humoral medicine, or eternal damnation.
*   **The Physicality of the Past:**
    *   *Sensory Realism:* The pervasive smell of woodsmoke and manure; the constant toothache; the utter, pitch darkness of night before gas/electric lighting.
    *   *Scarcity Economics:* Salt is a precious currency; cloth requires months of manual spinning; a winter freeze means genuine starvation.

---

## 2. Invariant Craft Rules for Historical Drama

1. **The Witness Protagonist Rule:**
   * Ground the narrative not in the legendary kings, but in the clerk, the archer, or the handmaiden standing at the edge of the royal tent. Their sensory vulnerability grounds history in visceral reality.
2. **The Incurious Insider applied to History:**
   * Characters do not describe the construction of their armor or saddles to themselves; they complain about saddle sores and rusted chainmail rivets.
