# Fair-Play Mystery Architecture

A true "Fair-Play" mystery is an intellectual game played between author and reader. The reader must have every piece of evidence necessary to solve the crime before the detective delivers the solution.

---

## 1. The Detection Club Golden Rules

1. **Early Introduction:** The culprit must be introduced in Act 1, appearing as an organic part of the world (never introduced in the final chapters).
2. **Strict Ban on Cheap Tricks:**
   * No unexplained poisons or exotic sci-fi tech unknown to science.
   * No supernatural cop-outs.
   * No accidental coincidences or "divine intuition" solving the case.
   * No unmentioned identical twins.
3. **Clue Transparency:** Every physical clue, timeline discrepancy, and spoken slip witnessed by the detective must be placed squarely on the page for the reader.

---

## 2. The Clue Ledger & Red Herring Matrix

In SAGA Stage 02 planning, maintain a **Clue Ledger**:
* **Real Clue:** The detail that points directly to the killer (e.g., mud on the carpet at 9:00 PM), disguised as mundane backdrop or emotional drama.
* **Red Herring (The Plausible Alternative):** A suspicious fact that legitimately incriminates an innocent suspect who is concealing an unrelated secret (e.g., embezzlement, an affair).
* **The "Hiding in Plain Sight" Rule:** Give clues dual utility. If a broken watch is crucial evidence of the time of death, ensure it is smashed during an emotional argument so the reader focuses on the character drama rather than the minute hand.
