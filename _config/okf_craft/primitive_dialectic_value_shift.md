# The Dialectic Value Shift: The Story Quantum

The smallest irreducible unit of story is not a word, a sentence, or a beat. It is a **Value Shift**: a measurable transition of a human condition from one state to its opposite.

If no value changes, **nothing happened**, no matter how many explosions or lines of dialogue were written.

---

## 1. The Core Human Value Polarities

Every scene must manipulate at least one primal human value across its polarity spectrum:

| Positive (+) | Negative (-) | Double Negative / Negation of the Negation (--) |
|---|---|---|
| **Life** | Death | A fate worse than death (Eternal torment / Damnation) |
| **Love** | Hate | Indifference disguised as love (Sociopathy / Betrayal) |
| **Truth** | Deception | Self-deception / The accepted systemic lie |
| **Justice** | Injustice | Tyranny disguised as law / Corrupted justice |
| **Freedom** | Captivity | Self-imposed enslavement / Institutional Stockholm syndrome |
| **Competence**| Failure | Arrogance causing catastrophic ruin to innocents |

---

## 2. Invariant Scene Anatomy (The Quantum Shift)

Every single scene must possess:
1. **Opening Value State:** The initial condition of the character/situation (e.g., Hopeful `[+]`).
2. **The Turning Force:** An obstacle, revelation, or active choice that challenges that condition.
3. **Closing Value State:** The irrevocable resulting state (e.g., Betrayed `[-]`).

A scene that opens on `Hopeful` and ends on `Hopeful` is an inert event. It must be cut or compressed into a transitional sentence.
