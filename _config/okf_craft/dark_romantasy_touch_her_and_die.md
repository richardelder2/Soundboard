# Dark Romantasy: Conventions & Reader Expectations

Dark Romantasy marries high-stakes fantasy worldbuilding with intense, morally complex romance. The genre's commercial dominance relies on strict delivery of psychological catharsis around power, protection, and dangerous passion.

---

## 1. Obligatory Reader Contracts

1. **The Morally Gray Love Interest:**
   * Ruthless to the outside world, feared by kings and monsters alike, but harboring a fiercely tender, vulnerable core reserved solely for the protagonist.
2. **The "Touch Her / Him and Die" Dynamic (Violent Devotion):**
   * A signature visceral payoff: when an antagonist threatens the romantic partner, all moral restraint evaporates into terrifying, catastrophic retribution.
3. **The Grudge / Enemies-to-Lovers Friction:**
   * Genuine ideological or bloodline conflict. The leads cannot simply "like" each other; they must be bound by duty to oppose each other, turning mutual attraction into treason against their own people.
4. **The Shadow Integration (Unapologetic Power):**
   * Unlike conventional romance where the heroine softens the beast, Dark Romantasy frequently involves the heroine embracing her own hidden darkness, magical lethality, or political ambition alongside her mate.

---

## 2. Cross-Genre Hybridization

*   **Dark Romantasy Sci-Fi:** A genetically modified warlord from an empire fleet bound to an insurgent pilot through an experimental neural link.
*   **Gothic Romantasy Noir:** A spectral mob boss who controls an undead syndicate falling for the mortal detective assigned to burn his territory down.
