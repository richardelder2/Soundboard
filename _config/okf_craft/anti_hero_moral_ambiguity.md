# The Anti-Hero Arc & Moral Ambiguity

An anti-hero arc rejects conventional moral purity, following a protagonist whose virtues and vices are deeply intertwined.

---

## 1. Engine of the Anti-Hero

*   **The Sympathetic Wound:** Their cynical worldview is not gratuitous; it was forged by a system or trauma where virtue was punished.
*   **The Pragmatic Code:** They possess strict internal ethics that directly contradict societal laws (e.g., will rob a corporation, but protects an orphan).
*   **The Escalating Compromise:** Each step toward their goal requires sacrificing an ethical boundary.

---

## 2. Five Movements of the Arc

1. **Flawed Equilibrium:** Operating successfully within their questionable niche.
2. **The Catalyst Dilemma:** An inciting incident that cannot be solved through their standard cynical shortcuts.
3. **The Line Crossed:** A moment where the protagonist commits a genuinely shocking or morally compromising act.
4. **The Mirror Crisis (The Point of No Return):** Forced to choose between self-preservation/profit and a selfless sacrifice.
5. **Resolution (The True Cost):**
   * *Variant A (Redemption through Ruin):* Saving others at the total destruction of their own life/standing.
   * *Variant B (Tragic Corruption):* Securing their external goal, but losing whatever remained of their soul.
