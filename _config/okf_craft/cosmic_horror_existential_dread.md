# Cosmic Horror & Epistemological Dread

Cosmic horror operates on a different axis than visceral horror (slashers, monsters). It is not about the threat of death, but the terrifying collapse of human significance and rational comprehension.

---

## 1. The Core Pillars of Cosmic Dread

*   **Radical Indifference:** The cosmos is not actively malicious; it simply does not care about humanity. Human morality and civilization are ephemeral bacteria on a rock.
*   **The Incomprehensible Scale:** Threats cannot be defeated with weapons or technology. Comprehending their geometry or reality shatters human cognition.
*   **Forbidden Knowledge (The Toxic Epiphany):** The protagonist's arc is not physical survival, but uncovering a truth that permanently destroys their sanity.

---

## 2. Invariant Craft Rules for Cosmic Horror

1. **The Negative Capability Rule:**
   * Never describe the entity's anatomy in full physiological detail. Over-description reduces a god to a giant squid. Describe the sensory aftermath, physical nausea, optical distortions, and mental panic.
2. **Futile Victory:**
   * If the protagonist survives, the "victory" is merely surviving long enough to realize that the cosmic cycle continues unaffected.
