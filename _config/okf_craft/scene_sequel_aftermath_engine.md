# The Scene-Sequel Aftermath Engine

Storytelling alternates between **Scenes** (proactive pursuit meeting disaster) and **Sequels** (reactive processing resolving into a new decision). Pacing breaks when aftermaths are skipped (causing emotional numbness) or bloated (causing narrative stall).

---

## 1. The Dual Phase Heartbeat

1. **The Scene:** Goal (Immediate) $\rightarrow$ Conflict (Escalating) $\rightarrow$ Disaster (Denial or Pyrrhic Win).
2. **The Sequel:** Reaction (Visceral shock) $\rightarrow$ Dilemma (Dwindling options) $\rightarrow$ Decision (New urgent commitment).
*The Sequel's decision becomes the next Scene's Goal.*

---

## 2. The Four Aftermath Steps

1. **Reaction (Visceral):** Adrenaline crash, physical shock, or grief before intellect returns.
2. **Processing (Cognitive):** Taking stock of losses, burned bridges, and time constraints.
3. **Dilemma (Pressure Cooker):** Weighing bad options under risk (*Option A costs reputation; Option B risks allies*).
4. **Decision & Changed Intent:** Committing to an immediate next step, launching the next scene.

---

## 3. Diagnosing Stagnant Scenes

* **Zero-Turn Trap:** Characters argue, discover nothing new, make no choice, and leave unchanged. **Fix:** Force a non-negotiable concession or deadline.
* **Skipped Sequel:** Non-stop explosions without absorbing emotional cost. **Fix:** Insert a 200-word processing beat.
* **Paralyzed Sequel:** Characters reflect for chapters without deciding. **Fix:** Add external clock forcing commitment.

---

## 4. Multi-Path Revision Options

* **Option A (Compress Sequel):** Condense internal lamentation into a visceral breath and an immediate choice.
* **Option B (Dramatize Dilemma):** Externalize debate into an argument with an ally who favors the competing choice.
* **Option C (Shift Exit):** End not on summary reflection, but on an irrevocable threshold crossing.
