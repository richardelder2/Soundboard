# Four-Corner Opposition & Foil Matrix

Cast ensembles operate within a dialectical ecosystem. Rather than flat two-pole conflict, Four-Corner Opposition and Foil Matrices ensure characters represent distinct, irreconcilable moral arguments competing for identical thematic ground.

---

## 1. Truby’s Four-Corner Opposition

```
[Corner 1: Protagonist] <=======> [Corner 2: Primary Opponent]
          ▲                                  ▲
          │                                  │
          ▼                                  ▼
[Corner 3: Secondary Opponent] <=> [Corner 4: Chaos Opponent]
```

1. **Corner 1 (Protagonist):** Pursues external goal driven by internal need; believes their moral approach is righteous.
2. **Corner 2 (Primary Opponent):** Competes directly for the exact same prize; embodies the most coherent philosophical counter-argument.
3. **Corner 3 (Secondary Opponent / Frenemy):** On the hero's side or institution; views hero as naive and urges cynical compromise.
4. **Corner 4 (Tertiary Opponent / Chaos):** Unpredictable, mercenary; attacks both hero and antagonist, exposing systemic fragility.

## 2. Thematic Value Matrix: "Justice in a Corrupt Empire"

| Corner | Character | Core Philosophy | Fatal Flaw |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Corner 1** | Protagonist (Rebel Scout) | "Justice requires tearing down corrupt law." | Naive recklessness; collateral damage. |
| **Corner 2** | Primary (Inquisitor) | "Order at any cost; chaos is worse than tyranny."| Dehumanization; ruthlessness. |
| **Corner 3** | Secondary (Commander) | "Sacrifice whoever necessary; morality is luxury." | Machiavellian corruption. |
| **Corner 4** | Tertiary (Smuggler) | "Every side is a scam; look out for yourself." | Isolation; betrayal of love. |

---

## 3. The Character Foil Matrix

Prevent voice homogenization across four behavioral axes:
1. **Status Posture:** Calm understated command vs. insecure blustering intimidation.
2. **Cognitive Tempo:** Sparse, analytical declarations vs. rapid intuitive tangents.
3. **Stress Defense:** Cold silent withdrawal vs. explosive dark humor.
4. **Moral Threshold:** Will lie but never kill vs. kills cleanly but never breaks an oath.

---

## 4. Cast Diagnostics Checklist

* [ ] **All corners competing for same prize?** Ensure stakes force inevitable collision.
* [ ] **Can any two characters swap lines?** If yes, combine them or sharpen moral polarity.
* [ ] **Do allies generate friction?** Allies must contest tactics, status, or ethical boundaries.
