# Narrative Tell Suppression & Dialectics

In human narrative, themes are proved by physical consequences and moral dilemmas, never stated by the narrator.

## Invariant Rules
1. **The Narrator Never States the Theme:** Never summarize the moral or meaning of an event in narration.
2. **Moralizing Tails Banned:** Strip closing statements that summarize "what was learned today."
3. **Show Physical Cost:** Philosophical conflicts must be embodied in irrevocable physical choices and sensory aftermath.
