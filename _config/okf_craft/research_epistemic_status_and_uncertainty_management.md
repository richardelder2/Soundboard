# Research, Fact & Uncertainty Management

When historical, procedural, scientific, or secondary-world lore enters a manuscript, authors and agents must distinguish **verified reality from creative invention**. Fabricating citations or projecting false certainty shatters reader trust and corrupts project canon.

---

## 1. The Six-Tier Epistemic Classification

Every piece of lore, historical claim, or world fact belongs to one of six explicit categories:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 1. Confirmed Research Fact : Verified by primary historical/scientific │
│                              consensus (e.g. 1874 Colt Peacemaker specs).│
│ 2. Established Canon Fact  : Verified in local canon.md / series_canon.│
│ 3. Plausible Inference     : Logical deduction consistent with era/world│
│                              laws, pending author verification.        │
│ 4. Intentional Invention   : Deliberate departure for dramatic effect  │
│                              (alternate history, magic systems).        │
│ 5. Open Research Question  : Explicit gap requiring archival research. │
│ 6. Contradictory Source    : Disputed claim across historical sources. │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

---

## 2. Anti-Hallucination Protocols for Agents

1. **Never Fabricate Sources:** When uncertain about a historical detail, date, or ballistic trajectory, state *"I do not know"* or mark the draft with `[RESEARCH: query]`. Never fabricate an archival source or museum citation.
2. **The Tagged Canon Protocol:** Any provisional world rule or timeline fact invented during drafting must be tagged as `[unverified chN]` in `canon.md`.
3. **Preserving Speculative License:** In historical fantasy, steampunk, or alternate history, deliberate historical anachronisms must be recorded in the planning bible to protect them from automated over-correction.

---

## 3. Research Diagnostic Checklist

* [ ] Are historical weapons, medical treatments, and legal procedures authenticated against era realities?
* [ ] Are deliberate historical divergences recorded in `canon.md` under `# In-World Inventions`?
* [ ] Are all provisional facts tagged with `[unverified chN]` before submitting to Stage 04 gate?

---

## 4. Multi-Path Revision Options

* **Option A (Verify & Anchor):** Check primary source; record verified detail in `canon.md`.
* **Option B (Acknowledge Historical Anachronism):** If the writer intentionally uses anachronistic elements, document the artistic license.
* **Option C (Oblique Framing):** If research is inconclusive, describe the scene through subjective sensory impact rather than technical metrics.
