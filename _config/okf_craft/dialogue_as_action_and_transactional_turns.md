# Dialogue as Action: Transactional Turning Points

Dramatic dialogue is never mere conversation; **dialogue is physical action executed through language**. Every line is a tactical maneuver designed to extract concessions, test loyalty, conceal vulnerability, shift status, or force commitment.

---

## 1. Five Transactional Currency Shifts

Every effective dialogue exchange must alter at least one relational ledger:
1. **Information Asymmetry:** Surrendering a secret, confirming a suspicion, planting a lie.
2. **Status Capital:** Claiming dominance, conceding territory, rejecting subordination.
3. **Commitment / Debt:** Extracting a promise, incurring a debt, breaking an alliance.
4. **Emotional Vulnerability:** Cracking armor; exposing fear, desire, or shame.
5. **Operational Strategy:** Abandoning, compromising, or replacing a plan under duress.

---

## 2. Anatomy of a Dialogue Turning Point

| Beat | Dramatic Function | Worked Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Opening Gambit** | Tactical objective pursued. | *"Sign the release before the noon courier leaves."* |
| **Active Pushback** | Challenges status or cost. | *"Your signature isn't on the audit, David. Why?"* |
| **Escalation** | Raises leverage or stakes. | *"If I sign, your father's foundation goes under with us."* |
| **Turning Point** | Irreversible concession/breach. | *She sets her pen down and folds her hands.* |
| **New Equilibrium** | Power dynamic permanently altered.| *"Tell the courier to wait until tomorrow."* |

---

## 3. Anti-Default Rules & Revision

* **Ban Neat Apologies:** Characters deflect, weaponize, or buy apologies with excuses.
* **Enforce Misdirection:** Characters evade dangerous inquiries by answering a question that wasn't asked.

**Multi-Path Revision:**
* **Option A (Heighten Stakes):** Give one speaker an urgent secret they must protect at all costs.
* **Option B (Asymmetric Wants):** Make Speaker A seek money while Speaker B seeks dignity.
* **Option C (Prune Consensus):** Cut reconciliation; end on an unresolved threat or pregnant silence.
