# Noir & Hardboiled Crime: The Fatalistic Engine

While classic whodunits focus on restoring moral order, **Noir** operates on the premise that the social order is already rotten to the core. Solving the crime does not fix the world.

---

## 1. Core Mechanics of Noir Fiction

*   **The Tainted Investigator:** The protagonist is not an untarnished Sherlock Holmes. They are compromised, broke, divorced, or traumatized, clinging to a personal, battered code of ethics in a corrupt city.
*   **The Labyrinthine Web (Institutional Complicity):** The murder investigation starts with a street-level delinquent, but climbing the ladder reveals that the police chief, the mayor, and the wealthy philanthropist are all complicit.
*   **The Femme Fatale / Fatal Catalyst:** A client or partner who uses emotional intimacy or financial desperation to lure the protagonist into a trap.
*   **The Pyrrhic Payoff:** The mystery is solved, but the killer cannot be legally touched, the innocent client is destroyed, or the detective is left spiritually bankrupt.

---

## 2. Cross-Genre Application

*   **Cyberpunk Noir (Blade Runner):** The cynical private eye navigating neon megacorp decay.
*   **Fantasy Noir:** A magical bounty hunter hired by an archmage to investigate a rune murder in a squalid goblin slum.
