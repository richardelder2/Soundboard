# Chapter Openings & Entry Vectors

A chapter opening must establish narrative traction within three sentences. Amateur openings linger in **throat-clearing rituals** (waking up, alarm clocks, mirrors, commuting). Professional openings deploy deliberate **entry vectors** matched to dramatic promise.

---

## 1. The Seven Entry Vectors

| Mode | Core Mechanism | Worked Example |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Disturbance** | Status quo disrupted mid-motion. | *"The third explosion took out the secondary relays."* |
| **2. Voice / Axiom** | Provocative, biased worldview statement. | *"No man ever confessed to murder with clean boots."* |
| **3. Immediate Desire** | Concrete hunger driving physical action. | *"Kael had twelve minutes to crack the lock or hang."* |
| **4. Consequence** | Waking inside yesterday's wreckage. | *"The blood on the kitchen tile had dried to dark lacquer."* |
| **5. Sensory Image** | Uncanny or tactile physical grounding. | *"The river smelled of vinegar and drowned willow roots."* |
| **6. Curiosity Gap** | Incongruous juxtaposition. | *"The bishop arrived with three rifles and no bible."* |
| **7. Spatial Framing** | Lean, active terrain orientation. | *"Winter had locked the harbor inside eight inches of gray ice."* |

---

## 2. Diagnostic Pitfalls & Checklist

1. **Throat-Clearing:** Waking up, stretching, hygiene. **Fix:** Cut to the catalyst.
2. **Dislocated Void:** Five paragraphs of dialogue without physical setting. **Fix:** Anchor coordinates by sentence two.
3. **Weather Cliché:** Meteorological description without character impact. **Fix:** Bind weather to physical discomfort.

* [ ] **First Sentence Motion:** Does sentence one contain an active verb or tangible consequence?
* [ ] **Traction Test:** Is character desire or immediate stakes clear before paragraph three?

---

## 3. Multi-Path Revision Options

* **Option A (Hard Trim):** Cut paragraphs 1–2; start at the first spoken word or physical collision.
* **Option B (Invert Sequence):** Pull a striking consequence from page three into paragraph one.
* **Option C (Voice Infusion):** Recast establishing description through focalizer bias.
