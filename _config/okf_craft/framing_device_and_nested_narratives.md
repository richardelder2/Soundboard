# Framing Devices & Nested Narratives

A framing device embeds an inner story inside an outer narrative context. This alters the reader's relationship to the tale by introducing mediation, memory decay, and strategic bias.

---

## 1. Functions of the Outer Frame

1. **The Life-or-Death Stake (The Scheherazade Engine):** The act of storytelling itself is high stakes. The storyteller must keep the listener captivated to prevent an execution, a murder, or a spiritual collapse.
2. **The Lens of Distance:** An old veteran recounting a war to a naive listener; the frame character's present-day cynicism colors the romanticism of the past.
3. **The Unreliable Narrator's Confession:** The frame is a legal interrogation, a deathbed confession, or an asylum interview where the narrator tells a story to justify a monstrous act.

---

## 2. Invariant Rules for Nested Structure

*   The outer frame must not be abandoned. The climax of the inner story must trigger a profound physical or moral reaction in the outer frame listener/teller.
