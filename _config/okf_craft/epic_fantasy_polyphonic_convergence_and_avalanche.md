# Epic Fantasy & Space Opera: Avalanche Convergence

Epic scale balances intimate character interiority with geopolitical consequences, slamming divergent viewpoint threads into a synchronized multi-front climax.

---

## 1. Non-Negotiable Reader Contracts

1. **Secondary-World Coherence:** Believable ecological, economic, and magical/technological consistency. Magic operates under known rules and costs—never an arbitrary escape hatch.
2. **Polyphonic Voice Integrity:** Distant POVs maintain distinct cadences, stakes, and agency. Every viewpoint must actively alter the world-state.
3. **Civilizational Transformation:** The climax permanently redraws geopolitical maps, arcane systems, or social contracts.

---

## 2. Obligatory Scene Ledger

1. **Cosmological Portent / Ancient Echo:** Opening sequence showing an ancient seal failing or existential anomaly awakening.
2. **Threshold Severing:** Protagonists torn from domestic baselines; return is physically or legally impossible.
3. **World Expansion & Political Fracture:** POV threads diverge across realms, exposing competing factions and dogmas.
4. **Council of Disunity:** Leaders gather to confront the crisis; ancient grievances prevent unified action.
5. **Midpoint Cataclysm:** A central fortress or capital falls, proving existing institutions helpless.
6. **The Abyss (Magic/Hope Broken):** Mentors fall; primary systems glitch; prophecies prove deceptive.
7. **Convergent Climax ("Avalanche"):** Far-flung POVs collide in a tightly synchronized temporal window where each thread enables the next.
8. **Costly Re-equilibrium:** The threat is transformed at immense cost, leaving the world permanently changed.

---

## 3. Pacing Acceleration: The Avalanche Structure

```
Acts I & IIa (0–60%): Expansive Chapters (3,500–5,000w)
  [POV A: Frontier] ---> [POV B: Court] ---> [POV C: Academy]

Act IIb (60–80%): Convergence Accelerates (2,000–3,000w)
  [A & B Cross Paths] ---> [C Deciphers Cipher] ---> [Armies Mobilize]

Act III (80–100%): Rapid Cross-Cutting (400–1,000w per perspective)
  [POV A: Breach Wall] ---> [POV B: Citadel Duel] ---> [POV C: Seal Ritual]
```

### Signature Trope Payoffs
* **Convergent Keystone:** An anomaly found by POV C early provides the exact key POV A needs in the climax.
* **Hard Magic Sacrifice:** Climax turns on exploiting established limitations/costs codified early, purchased through genuine sacrifice.
