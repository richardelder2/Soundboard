# Voice Differentiation: Engineering Non-Homogeneous Cast Registers

The single most prevalent AI failure in multi-character dialogue is **Homogeneous Register Syndrome**: every character speaks with identical syntax lengths, vocabulary sophistication, polite turn-taking, and complete grammatically balanced sentences.

Human speech is asymmetric, tribal, fragmented, and filtered through individual psychology.

---

## 1. The Idiolect Matrix: 5 Dials per Character

Configure every primary and secondary character along these five distinct linguistic dials:

| Dial | Low Setting (-) | High Setting (+) |
|---|---|---|
| **Sentence Length & Cadence** | Fragmented, staccato, telegraphic ("Done. Next.") | Hypotactic, subordinating, baroque ("Given that the..." ) |
| **Vocabulary Register** | Concrete, tactile, vulgar, monosyllabic | Abstract, bureaucratic, technical, latinates |
| **Interruption Bias** | Waits for silence, yields turn readily | Overlaps, cuts others off, finishes sentences |
| **Indirectness / Subtext** | Direct literal statements, blunt answers | Oblique deflections, ironic questions, answering with stories |
| **Filler / Tick Markers** | Zero verbal tics, austere pauses | Distinctive regionalisms, vocalized hesitations, specialized jargon |

---

## 2. Perceptual Filters: What Does Each Character Notice?

Voice is not just how a character speaks; it is **what they pay attention to** when entering a space:
*   *The Veteran:* Exits, line of sight, dominant hand positions, posture tension.
*   *The Architect:* Ceiling height, joinery quality, building materials, lighting angles.
*   *The Swindler:* Ring brands, watches, hesitations, social vulnerabilities, who looks to whom before speaking.

When drafting third-person limited or first-person, filter 100% of sensory intake through that character's professional and emotional neurosis.

---

## 3. Dialogue Blind Test Verification

**The Mechanical Gate Test:** Copy 20 lines of dialogue from an ensemble scene and strip all character tags ("he said", "she said").
*   If an objective reader cannot identify who is speaking each line with >85% accuracy based solely on rhythm, word choice, and attitude, the scene fails voice differentiation.
