# Textual Actual Worlds (TAW) & Modal Narratology

In modal cognitive narratology, a fictional story is not a single flat timeline. It is an **entire multiverse of competing subjective world-models** revolving around an objective ontological core called the **Textual Actual World (TAW)**.

Narrative suspense, dramatic irony, character agency, and emotional resonance are generated by the **mathematical discrepancy** between what exists in TAW and what characters *believe*, *wish*, or *fear* exists.

---

## 1. The Ontological Architecture of Narrative Reality

```
                          [TEXTUAL ACTUAL WORLD (TAW)]
                      (Objective Physical/Historical Facts)
                                       ▲
                   ┌───────────────────┼───────────────────┐
                   ▼                   ▼                   ▼
            [K-WORLDS]            [W-WORLDS]          [O-WORLDS]
        (Knowledge / Belief)     (Wishes / Desires)   (Obligations / Duties)
```

### 1. TAW (Textual Actual World)
- **Definition:** The objective, unvarnished physical and historical facts of the fictional universe as established by canonical narrative authority.
- **Craft Rule:** TAW is the ground truth. Characters can be mistaken about TAW, but TAW itself is immutable until changed by physical actions.

### 2. K-Worlds (Knowledge / Epistemic Worlds)
- **Definition:** What an individual character *believes* to be true about TAW.
- **The Epistemic Gap:**
  - When $K_{\text{Protagonist}} = \text{TAW}$, the character has perfect clarity.
  - When $K_{\text{Protagonist}} \neq \text{TAW}$, the character operates under delusion, mistaken identity, or enemy gaslighting.
  - When $K_{\text{Reader}} > K_{\text{Protagonist}}$, the gap produces **Suspense & Dramatic Irony**.

### 3. W-Worlds (Wish / Telic Worlds)
- **Definition:** The ideal world the character *wants* to actualize (their hopes, ambitions, and dreams).
- **The Want Engine:** Plot begins the moment a character perceives a painful delta between their wish and current reality:
  $$\Delta_{\text{Drive}} = W_{\text{Hero}} - \text{TAW}$$

### 4. O-Worlds (Obligation / Deontic Worlds)
- **Definition:** The laws, vows, oaths, social expectations, and moral codes imposed upon the character by their society, guild, or conscience.
- **The Moral Dilemma:** When $W_{\text{Hero}}$ (what they desire) conflicts violently with $O_{\text{Hero}}$ (what honor or survival demands), the character enters psychological crisis.

---

## 2. The Mechanics of Narrative Turning Points

### A. The TAW Boundary Crossing (Act I Break)
The protagonist physically or socially crosses a threshold into the opponent's territory. This is an **irrecoverable modal commitment**: once crossed, their previous lifestyle in TAW is deleted, and they cannot return to their original K-World safety.

### B. The TAW Reality Inversion (The Midpoint & Climax)
A devastating plot twist occurs when a character discovers that their entire K-World was an illusion.
- *Examples:*
  - *The Empire Strikes Back:* Luke believes Vader murdered his father ($K \neq \text{TAW}$). Vader reveals the horrifying ground truth ($K \leftarrow \text{TAW}$).
  - *The Sixth Sense:* Malcolm discovers he is dead.
  - *The Matrix:* Neo discovers the "real world" of 1999 is a simulated K-world, and TAW is a post-apocalyptic machine wasteland.

## 3. The Modal Narrative Audit Matrix

When diagnosing flat scenes, audit the modal discrepancies across characters:

| Diagnostic Metric | Check | Failure Mode (AI Tell) |
|---|---|---|
| **Epistemic Divergence** | Does each character in the room believe something slightly different about TAW? | All characters possess uniform, identical information (flat dialogue). |
| **Telic-Deontic Friction** | Does what the character wants ($W$) conflict with what duty demands ($O$)? | Characters happily do their duty without personal sacrifice or internal friction. |
| **K-World Resistance** | Does the character stubbornly resist updating their beliefs when TAW shows contrary proof? | Instant, emotionless logical updates without human denial or cognitive dissonance. |
