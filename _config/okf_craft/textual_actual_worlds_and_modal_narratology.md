# Textual Actual Worlds (TAW) & Modal Narratology

In modal narratology, story is a multiverse of competing subjective models around an objective core: the **Textual Actual World (TAW)**. Dramatic tension stems from the discrepancy between TAW and what characters believe, desire, or fear.

---

## 1. Ontological Architecture

```
                          [TEXTUAL ACTUAL WORLD (TAW)]
                      (Objective Physical/Historical Facts)
                                       ▲
                   ┌───────────────────┼───────────────────┐
                   ▼                   ▼                   ▼
            [K-WORLDS]            [W-WORLDS]          [O-WORLDS]
        (Knowledge / Belief)     (Wishes / Desires)   (Obligations / Duties)
```

### 1. TAW (Textual Actual World)
* **Definition:** Objective facts of the universe established by canonical narrative authority. Ground truth.

### 2. K-Worlds (Epistemic Worlds)
* **Definition:** What a character *believes* true about TAW.
* When $K_{\text{Hero}} \neq \text{TAW}$, character acts under delusion or deception.
* When $K_{\text{Reader}} > K_{\text{Hero}}$, produces **Dramatic Irony**.

### 3. W-Worlds (Telic Worlds)
* **Definition:** What the character *wishes* to actualize.
* **Want Engine:** Plot drives from the delta: $\Delta_{\text{Drive}} = W_{\text{Hero}} - \text{TAW}$.

### 4. O-Worlds (Deontic Worlds)
* **Definition:** Vows, laws, and moral codes imposed by society or conscience.
* **Dilemma:** Crisis erupts when $W_{\text{Hero}}$ conflicts with $O_{\text{Hero}}$.

---

## 2. Mechanics of Turning Points

* **TAW Boundary Crossing (Act I Break):** Protagonist commits across a threshold; old life in TAW deleted.
* **TAW Reality Inversion (Midpoint/Climax):** Devastating discovery that K-World was an illusion.
  * *Empire Strikes Back:* Luke learns Vader is his father ($K \leftarrow \text{TAW}$).
  * *Matrix:* 1999 is a simulated K-World; TAW is machine wasteland.

---

## 3. Modal Narrative Audit Matrix

| Metric | Check | Failure Mode (AI Tell) |
|---|---|---|
| **Epistemic Divergence** | Does each character believe something different about TAW? | All characters share uniform information (flat dialogue). |
| **Telic-Deontic Friction** | Does desire ($W$) conflict with duty ($O$)? | Characters execute duty without personal friction. |
| **K-World Resistance** | Does character resist updating beliefs when TAW contradicts? | Instant emotionless logical update without denial. |
