# Surreal Atmosphere & Dream Logic

Surreal storytelling does not mean random silliness. It is the systematic application of dream logic, sensory transpositions, and subtle ontological instability to evoke psychological truth.

---

## 1. The Mechanics of Dream Logic

*   **Internal Consistency:** The rules of the surreal world must be utterly rigid, even if they make no scientific sense. Characters treat impossible phenomena with bureaucratic indifference or quiet dread.
*   **Synesthesia & Sensory Crossing:** Describing sounds as textures, memories as scents, and emotional weights as physical barometric pressure.
*   **Spatial Slippage:** Rooms that are slightly larger on the inside; hallways that stretch imperceptibly when a character is in grief; doors that open into different weather.

---

## 2. Pacing the Destabilization

1. **The Subtle Wrongness:** Begin in a mundane, concrete setting with a single off-key sensory anomaly (a shadow leaning into the light; a clock ticking in triplets).
2. **The Rationalization Failure:** The protagonist attempts to explain the anomaly using conventional logic, but reality continues to drift.
3. **Total Submersion:** The metaphorical and internal psychology of the character becomes the literal external landscape.
