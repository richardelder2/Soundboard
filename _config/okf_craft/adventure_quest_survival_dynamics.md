# The Adventure Quest & Survival Mechanics

Adventure is defined by physical friction against hostile environments. Unlike static drama, adventure narratives derive momentum from **geographic progress** and **resource depletion**.

---

## 1. The Survival Attrition Engine

*   **The Depleting Inventory Ledger:** An adventure story must track resources with physical honesty: water canteens running dry, ropes snapping, ammunition dwindling, shoes wearing through.
*   **The Environment as Primary Antagonist:** Mountains, swamps, blizzards, desert salt flats, or radioactive wastelands are not passive backgrounds. They actively tax character stamina, body temperature, and morale.
*   **The Companion Fracture Point:** Extreme physical deprivation strips away polite team masks. Alliances fracture over a single ration bar or whether to carry a wounded member.

---

## 2. Cross-Genre Hybridization

*   **Sci-Fi Survival Quest (The Martian):** A single engineer battling Martian atmospheric chemistry using thermodynamics as a survival weapon.
*   **Historical Adventure:** An Arctic expedition crew navigating icepack entrapment while scurvy and paranoia set in.
