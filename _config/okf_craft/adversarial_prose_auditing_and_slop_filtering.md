# Adversarial Prose Auditing: The Anti-Slop & Anti-Filter Engine

High-viscosity prose is achieved by aggressively eliminating clichéd emotional postures, stripping indirect sensory filters, and anchoring every scene in concrete physical physics.

---

## 1. Slop Word Density Gate (< 5.0 / 1000 words)

Zero tolerance for worn-out thriller and romance crutches:
*   **Banned Postures & Clichés:**
    *   *Bodily Slop:* "shuddered", "sigh of relief", "cold sweat", "heart hammered", "breath caught", "eyes narrowed", "jaw clenched", "bile rose".
    *   *Smug AI Tells:* "smirk", "grinned mischievously", "a beat passed", "testament to", "delve".
    *   *Weak Qualifiers:* "suddenly", "almost", "somehow", "began to", "started to".

---

## 2. POV Camera Filter Stripping (< 12.0 / 1000 words)

A major AI and amateur tell is wrapping every sensory observation in a character filter, which creates emotional distance:

*   **Banned Filters:** "saw", "heard", "felt", "noticed", "spotted", "watched", "smelled", "tasted", "seemed", "looked", "thought", "believed", "realized".
*   **The Invariant Transformation:**
    *   *Weak Filtered:* Jax saw the bulkhead door shudder and heard the alarm klaxon scream.
    *   *High-Viscosity Unfiltered:* The bulkhead door shuddered against its hydraulic seals. The alarm klaxon tore through the corridor at 110 decibels.

---

## 3. Sensory Anchor Density (Minimum 3+ per chapter)

Every chapter must plant non-visual sensory anchors:
*   **Auditory:** Low-frequency turbine rumble, magnetic relay clicks, acoustic hollows.
*   **Olfactory:** Scorched wire insulation, ozone, stagnant hydraulic grease, dry copper.
*   **Thermal / Tactile:** Freezing vacuum conduct on steel deck plates vs. blistering exhaust backwash.
