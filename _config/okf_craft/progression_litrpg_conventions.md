# Progression Fantasy & LitRPG: Conventions & Reader Expectations

Progression Fantasy and LitRPG tap into the deep human drive for **measurable mastery and competence**. The central pleasure is witnessing deliberate effort, ingenuity, and discipline translate into tangible power.

---

## 1. Obligatory Reader Contracts

1. **Clear, Transparent Power Metrics:**
   * Whether numerical (levels, stats, ranks) or conceptual (martial cultivation stages, magic rings), the rules of advancement must be transparent and consistent. Advancement can never feel like an unearned handout or arbitrary plot armor.
2. **The Underdog / Systematic Disadvantage:**
   * The protagonist begins with a perceived flaw, an unconventional specialization, or zero magical affinity, forcing them to survive through lateral thinking, extreme work ethic, or deep system comprehension.
3. **The Breakthrough Catharsis (The Payoff):**
   * The climax of an arc must showcase the protagonist utilizing their hard-won competence to overcome an adversary or institution that previously humiliated or oppressed them.
4. **Escalating Cosmology:**
   * Each major milestone breakthrough reveals a wider, more dangerous world beyond (e.g., leaving the starting village to enter the regional sect, then the heavenly realm).

---

## 2. Cross-Genre Hybridization

*   **Cyberpunk Progression:** An impoverished decker systematically hacking firmware, upgrading neural wetware, and climbing the underground network hierarchy.
*   **Historical Progression:** A Renaissance apprentice mastering metallurgy and ballistics, rising from forge boy to chief engineer of an empire.
