# The Typology of Moral Degeneration

Characters descend through an escalating series of small moral compromises, each justified by an internal rationalization engine, until retreat becomes impossible.

---

## 1. Five Benchmark Degeneration Models

| Model | Avatar | Baseline | Rationalization Engine | Point of No Return | Terminal Calcification |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Tragic Hubris & Licensing** | **Walter White** (*Breaking Bad*) | Humiliated genius | *"Doing this strictly for family survival."* | Watching an innocent overdose to maintain control. | Narcissistic despotism ("Heisenberg"); total ego inflation. |
| **2. Cynical Armor & Grief Avoidance** | **Jimmy McGill** (*Better Call Saul*) | Underdog seeking approval | *"The legitimate game is rigged; shortcuts level the field."* | Career destruction resulting in Howard's death. | Amoral persona ("Saul Goodman"); severed intimacy. |
| **3. Dynastic Calcification** | **Michael Corleone** (*Godfather II*) | War hero resisting corruption | *"Must protect the Family as an institution."* | Ordering cold execution of his biological brother. | Machiavellian isolation; spiritual entombed silence. |
| **4. Therapeutic Sociopathy** | **Tony Soprano** (*The Sopranos*) | Anxious capo seeking relief | *"Prisoner of mother's trauma and genetics."* | Murdering loyal protégé to eliminate inconvenience. | Sociopathic calcification; therapy converted to extortion craft. |
| **5. Competitive Cannibalism** | **Daniel Plainview** (*There Will Be Blood*) | Driven pioneer | *"I have a competition in me; want no one else to succeed."* | Murdering imposter brother. | Raving misanthropic madness alone in private mansion. |

---

## 2. Five Structural Stations of Descent

```
[1. Baseline] ---> [2. Inciting Compromise] ---> [3. Licensing Escalation]
                                                               │
                                                               ▼
[5. Terminal Calcification] <--- [4. Point of No Return] ──────┘
```

1. **Baseline Integrity & Core Wound:** Sympathetic start carrying an unhealed psychological wound (humiliation, rejection).
2. **Inciting Compromise (Justified Exception):** First ethical breach framed as an emergency transaction to preserve life or family.
3. **Moral Licensing Escalation:** Success via initial crime leads to new threats; rationalization engine blames enemies for subsequent atrocities.
4. **Point of No Return (Soul Murder):** Clear choice between confessing or committing an irreversible betrayal. Self-preservation chosen.
5. **Terminal Calcification:** External victory achieved; internal emotional and moral capacity turned to stone.

---

## 3. Diagnostic Checklist

* [ ] **First crime sympathetic?** Avoid starting with sadism; the first compromise must feel unavoidable.
* [ ] **Transgressions chain causally?** Crime $N$ creates forensic or relational fallout forcing crime $N+1$.
* [ ] **Final reckoning stripped bare?** Protective rationalization must collapse before the end, exposing raw ego.
