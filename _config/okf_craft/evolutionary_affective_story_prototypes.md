# Affective Narratology & Evolutionary Story Prototypes

In Patrick Colm Hogan's **Affective Narratology**, cross-cultural narrative architectures are governed by universal neuro-emotional systems rooted in human evolutionary biology.

---

## 1. The Three Pan-Cultural Story Prototypes

Hogan identifies three universal story architectures found across documented human cultures:

### 1. The Heroic Prototype (In-Group Defense & Survival)
* **Evolutionary Imperative:** Defense of kin-group against external predators and physical annihilation.
* **Narrative Arc:**
  1. *Threat:* External predator or hostile force breaches the group's sanctuary.
  2. *Trial:* Designated defender undergoes trials proving combat competence and loyalty.
  3. *Confrontation:* Direct, high-stakes clash against the existential danger.
  4. *Resolution:* Perimeter secured, communal resources preserved, defender's status elevated.

### 2. The Romantic Prototype (Pair-Bonding & Social Continuity)
* **Evolutionary Imperative:** Sexual selection, overcoming reproductive barriers, and forming stable pair-bonds amidst clan divisions.
* **Narrative Arc:**
  1. *Attraction:* Mutual recognition of emotional or spiritual complementarity.
  2. *Social Divide:* Class hierarchies, family vendettas, or rival suitors block union.
  3. *Separation & Tribulation:* Lovers endure physical distance or mortal trials.
  4. *Resolution:* Barrier dissolved; union achieved and communally validated.

### 3. The Sacrificial Prototype (Communal Crisis & Scapegoat Renewal)
* **Evolutionary Imperative:** Resolving internal decay, civil factionalism, famine, or plague threatening the collective whole.
* **Narrative Arc:**
  1. *Blight:* Society suffers moral corruption or ecological collapse beyond normal law.
  2. *Guilt Identified:* Crisis demands supreme personal or ritual restitution.
  3. *Offering:* A noble agent accepts exile, suffering, or death to purge the corruption.
  4. *Resolution:* Equilibrium restored through the cathartic sacrifice of the individual.

## 2. Somatosensory Appraisal: The Cognitive Emotion Cycle

Emotions are rapid somatic programs prioritizing goals before conscious thought:

```
[Sensory Registration] ---> [Affective Appraisal] ---> [Actional Orientation]
```

### Craft Rule for Prose:
When characters encounter shocks, avoid abstract emotion labels (*"He felt terrified"*). Trace the evolutionary cycle:
1. **Sensory Cue:** Metallic scrape of a latch, drop in room temperature.
2. **Somatic Spike:** Adrenaline dumping into chest, constricted throat, raised arm hair.
3. **Action Impulse:** Bracing against wall, weight shifting to balls of feet, locking eyes on exit.
