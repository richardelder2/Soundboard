# The Eight Strata of Revision

Attempting to fix prose cadence, plot causality, character voice, and theme simultaneously causes cognitive paralysis and wasted labor. Polishing sentences in a scene that must be deleted is the most expensive mistake in publishing. **Revision must descend through eight distinct strata.**

---

## 1. The Eight Revision Strata & Triage Order

```
[Strata 1: Premise & Core Promise]       ──> High Leverage (Macro Structure)
[Strata 2: Causality & Plot Logic]       ──>
[Strata 3: Character Want, Need & Agency]──>
[Strata 4: Relationship Systems & Power] ──>
───────────────────────────────────────────── (Pass Line: Do not edit prose above this line)
[Strata 5: Scene Structure & Pacing]     ──> Intermediate Units
[Strata 6: POV, Focalization & Voice]    ──>
[Strata 7: Description, Sensory & Prose] ──> Micro Mechanics
[Strata 8: Cadence, Repetition & Copy]   ──> Final Polish
```

| Strata | What It Evaluates | Common Symptoms | What NOT to Fix at this Layer |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Premise** | Reader contract, genre obligations, hook payoff. | Sagging second act, unfulfilled pitch promises. | Do not tweak individual scene dialogue. |
| **2. Causality** | Cause-and-effect chain, timeline, coincidence traps. | Plot holes, lucky coincidences, unearned clues. | Do not polish prose cadence. |
| **3. Character** | Want vs Need, active agency, coherent change. | Passive protagonist, unmotivated moral reversals. | Do not fuss with paragraph lengths. |
| **4. Relational** | Dyadic power balance, shifting status, subtext. | Interchangeable cast reactions, flat alliances. | Do not fix spelling or minor word repetitions. |
| **5. Scene** | 5 Commandments, value shifts, scene/sequel balance. | Zero-turn scenes, excessive logistical travel. | Do not rewrite sentences that will be cut. |
| **6. POV / Voice** | Epistemic horizons, psychic distance, idiolects. | Accidental head-hopping, tell-heavy internal summaries. | Do not alter macro plot turns. |
| **7. Sensory / Prose** | Concrete imagery, anti-slop filters, authentic tags. | Camera-eye inventory, stock clichés (*cold sweat*). | Do not reorganize the chapter outline. |
| **8. Cadence / Copy** | Sentence CV, acoustic rhythm, copyedit errors. | Monotonous lengths, word echoes, typos. | Do not introduce new plot developments. |

---

## 2. The Golden Law of Upstream Precedence

* If an issue exists at Strata 2 (Causality), never resolve it with a Strata 7 (Prose) patch.
* Never rewrite a line before confirming the scene containing it will survive the Strata 1–4 passes.

---

## 3. Multi-Path Revision Protocol

When auditing a manuscript, classify findings by their stratum number. Present structural options before prose line adjustments.
