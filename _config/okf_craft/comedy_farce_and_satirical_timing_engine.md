# Architecture of Comedy, Farce & Satirical Timing

Literary comedy is an algorithmic system of structural pressure, spatial confinement, perceptual blinders, and status friction.

---

## 1. Foundational Engines: Kaplan & Vorhaus

$$\text{Comedy} = \text{An Ordinary Person Struggling Against Insurmountable Odds Without Tools, Never Giving Up Hope}$$

* **Non-Hero & Earnest Action:** Characters never try to be funny; they act with passionate sincerity pursuing flawed internal logic.
* **Straight Line / Wavy Line:** The Straight Line generates chaos oblivious to catastrophe; the Wavy Line scrambles in mounting panic witnessing it.
* **Vorhaus Comic Instrument:** (1) Core Flaw/Obsession, (2) Wild-Card Coping Habit, (3) Unbending Environment punishing the flaw.

---

## 2. Incongruity & Register Ruptures

* **Wodehouse Transferred Epithet (Hypallage):** Projecting emotion onto inert objects (*"He smoked a meditative cigarette and regarded the butler with an apprehensive eyebrow"*).
* **Wodehouse Register Rupture:** Wrapping trivial social crises in epic Latinate diction, then puncturing with vulgar reality.
* **Pratchett Anthropomorphic Grounding:** Subjecting mythic absolutes (Death, Fate) to municipal bureaucracy and union rules.
* **Literalized Metaphor:** Treating common idioms literally (*"searching for his wits"* on hands and knees under floorboards).

---

## 3. The Farcical Pressure Cooker

```
Spatial Confinement + Ticking Clock + Asymmetric Lies + Panic Cover-ups
                                │
                                ▼
         [CATASTROPHIC CONVERGENCE: All parties enter one room]
```

1. **Lie Plant:** Minor falsehood to protect pride.
2. **Acceleration:** Compounding lies; darting through opposite doors seconds apart.
3. **Catastrophic Convergence:** All deceived parties meet in one room, stripping facades.

---

## 4. Rule of Three & Callback Engine

* **Micro Rule of Three:** Beat 1 (Establishment) $\rightarrow$ Beat 2 (Confirmation) $\rightarrow$ Beat 3 (Subversive Inversion / Peripeteia).
  * *Ex:* *"Mastered international law... studied Vienna dialects... trained his terrier to bite French passports."*
* **Callback Engine:** Seed eccentric detail in Act I $\rightarrow$ incubate in Act II $\rightarrow$ harvest in Act III as accidental linchpin resolving crisis.

---

## 5. Status Inversions

* **High-Status Buffoon:** Authority figure sweating with low-status panic.
* **Low-Status Sovereign:** Servant exercising absolute calm and control.
* **Payoff:** Forcing the pompous authority to physically beg the subordinate.
