# Procedural Dread & Administrative Terror

The most chilling horror does not come from screaming monsters; it comes from cold, polite, institutional indifference where atrocity is rendered in sterile bureaucratic language.

---

## 1. The Mechanics of Administrative Horror

*   **The Sterile Euphemism:** Replace horrific physical violence with clinical corporate acronyms (e.g., *End of Life (EOL) Protocol*, *Biomass Reclamation*, *Redistribution*, *Disciplinary Audit*).
*   **The "Decode Once" Rule:** Explain the grotesque reality behind the euphemism in visceral detail exactly **once** early in the narrative. Thereafter, use the sterile corporate term without explanation. The reader's memory of the horror makes the polite term agonizingly terrifying.
*   **Administrative Cut-Off at Death:**
    *   Never depict the cinematic moment of death with gore or theatrical screams.
    *   Cut off the victim's internal monologue mid-thought.
    *   Confirm the fatality through an administrative system log (e.g., `coverage interrupted`, `telemetry lost at 04:12:09`, `unit reassigned`).

---

## 2. Invariant Restraint Rules

*   **Banning Melodrama:** Characters under institutional doom do not give heroic speeches; they comply with paperwork, adjust uniforms, or worry about missing pens.
*   **The Cold Voice of the State:** Corporate or totalitarian authority speaks in the passive voice ("Mistakes were registered," "Measures have been authorized"), eliminating personal responsibility.
