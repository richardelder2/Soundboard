# The Full Manuscript Continuity Pass

When a novel draft is complete, story-level continuity issues must be resolved systematically before performing line-level prose editing. A continuity pass is strictly structural, never a prose polish.

---

## 1. The Isolation & Ripple Sequencing Protocol

1. **Topological Geography First:**
   * If a fix alters the physical layout, travel times, or geography of a core hub (e.g., station layout, castle floorplan), execute this fix **first as an isolated pass**. Everything downstream depends on space and travel physics.
2. **Sequential Entity Checks:**
   * Numeric attributes (e.g., character ages, dates, distances) must be audited across the entire manuscript with zero tolerance for drift.
3. **Causal Transaction Integrity:**
   * Trace physical trade items and economy: if an item is stolen or bartered in Chapter 12, verify that its appearance or exchange in Chapter 15 honors the emotional stakes and realistic value established earlier.
4. **Retroactive Foreshadowing Insertions:**
   * If a late-appearing character (e.g., an ally in Chapter 19) is crucial to the climax, insert a single concrete beat of competence in Act 1 or Act 2 to establish reader recognition before their major role.
