# Character Arc Map

*Arcs are interior structure — they need scheduling and auditing exactly like obligatory scenes, or the draft delivers plot without change. One sheet per arcing character. Stage 03 drafts the beats where scheduled; Stage 04 audits delivery alongside the trope ledger.*

## Arc taxonomy (pick per character)

- **Positive change (upward):** believes a lie → painful movement toward truth → climax choice embodies truth. The commercial default.
- **Flat (testing):** already holds the truth; the story pressures them to abandon it; they pay to hold it and change *others*. The natural arc for series leads, cozy sleuths, and progression MCs.
- **Disillusionment (downward, wiser):** believes a comforting lie → discovers a darker truth → ends knowing more and happier never again. Folk horror and noir live here.
- **Corruption (downward, fall):** sees the truth clearly → wants something more → rationalizes step by step → rejects truth at the climax. Requires a visible rationalization ladder.
- **Static:** no arc — a fixed point others move around. Valid for chorus and some antagonists; never for the protagonist by accident.

**Anti-default check (mandatory):** an all-positive ensemble is the AI default. Generate three arc-type assignments for the cast, discard the first (per structure_plan §10), and justify the mix. Genre contract still rules: romance leads need mutually completing arcs; a progression MC is usually flat; horror may go down.

### Authenticity constraints on this arc
- The narrator NEVER names the lie, the truth, or the lesson — beats land through choice and consequence (`_config/narrative_authenticity.md`, Layer 1: Theme).
- At least one arc beat should cut against sympathy (the morally condemnable decision from structure_plan §5 can double as beat 6 or 7).
- Beat 9 must be an action or image, not reflection. "She understood now that…" fails the audit.

- Antagonist has a sheet or an explicit `static` designation with motive; "evil because plot" fails Stage 04.
