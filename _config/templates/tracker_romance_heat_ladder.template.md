# Romance & Heat Escalation Ladder

*Romantic chemistry cannot jump instantaneously from hostility to unconditional devotion. It must ascend an **unyielding ladder of emotional vulnerability and physical heat**. In romantasy, dark romance, and rom-coms, this ledger ensures pacing discipline and guarantees that every touch, confession, and grovel is earned.*

---

## 1. Spice & Heat Calibration
- **Target Heat Level:** `[Closed Door (Sweet) / Warm (Fade-to-Black) / Explicit (On-Page Heat)]`
- **Pacing Rule:** Physical escalation must NEVER outpace emotional risk. A physical leap without emotional vulnerability feels clinical; an emotional confession without physical tension feels static.
- **Series Constraint (if series):** Max 1–2 major relationship rungs per book in slow burns (e.g., cozy mystery or 5-book dark romance).
