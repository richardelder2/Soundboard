# Lore-Debt & Narrative Promise Ledger

*In elite speculative and commercial fiction, every mysterious artifact, named-but-unseen room, ancient prophecy, buried family secret, and offhand magical rule is a **debt incurred with the reader**. Unpaid lore debts at the climax feel like broken contracts. Track every plant from the moment it is drafted until it is paid in full.*

---

## 2. Status Definitions & Audit Rules

*   **Open:** Planted on page; no explanation or payoff yet provided.
*   **Glimpsed:** Partial information or false lead given in an intermediate chapter (keeps the debt warm without paying it off).
*   **Paid:** Resolved on page with full dramatic or evidentiary clarity.
*   **Deliberately Unresolved:** Formally scheduled as a lingering question or atmospheric loose end per `_config/narrative_authenticity.md` (must be noted in `structure_plan.md`).

---

## 3. Series Promotion Protocol

When a novel completes Stage 04, any unresolved debts marked `Series Scope: Yes` must be promoted to the sibling `series/series_canon.md` and shared series trackers before Book N+1 begins. Never let a debt vanish into the void between books.
