# Sensory Lexicon & Visceral Vocabulary Guide

*Curated sensory vocabulary designed to eliminate generic descriptions and maintain visceral texture.*

---

## 1. Olfactory & Ambient Scent Palette
- **Industrial / Machine:** Scorched motor-windings, rancid tallow grease, sweet ester fumes, ozone after rain.
- **Organic & Biological:** Wet wolfhound fur, bruised pennyroyal, sour goat milk, marsh gas, dried marrow.
- **Culinary & Domestic:** Charred chicory root, stale clove water, sour dough crust, woodsmoke in damp wool.

---

## 2. Acoustic & Auditory Soundscape
- **Mechanical & Structural:** Screech of dry bearings, muffled turbine thrum, rivet groan, steam-hammer cadence.
- **Flesh & Movement:** Squelch of saturated boots, dry cartilage pop, ragged double-breath, raspy whisper.
- **Atmospheric:** Rain hammering slate roofs, wind screaming through cable stays, dead silence after snowfall.

---

## 3. Tactile, Texture & Thermal Palette
- **Surfaces & Textures:** Cold pitting on salt-eaten steel, greased rawhide, coarse burlap, flaking limewash.
- **Thermal Somatics:** Numbing needle-frost on earlobes, humid air like warm soup, greasy fever sweat.

---

## 4. Optical, Light & Color Palette
- **Lighting Dynamics:** Sodium-vapor amber, dull pewter overcast, gaslight flickering against green mold.
- **Palette Restrictions:** Banish "bright", "dark", "blue", "red"—use *vitriol green, bruised tallow, rusted zinc, oxidized copper*.

---

## 5. Banned AI Clichés & Preferred Replacements
| Banned Cliché | Why It Fails | Preferred Visceral Replacement |
|---|---|---|
| *"A testament to..."* | Abstract narration | Describe the crumbling stone or rusted bolt directly. |
| *"A tapestry of..."* | Pretentious metaphor | Name the specific competing smells or sounds. |
| *"A sudden chill ran down her spine."* | Melodramatic cliché | *"The back of her neck went cold and prickled against her wool collar."* |
