# Fair-Play Clue & Suspect Matrix

*In fair-play whodunits, psychological thrillers, and procedural mysteries, the plot is a **game of intellectual combat between the author and the reader**. The golden law: the reader must be given all the pieces required to solve the puzzle before the protagonist announces the solution. No phantom twins, no unmentioned poisons, and no clues discovered off-page.*
