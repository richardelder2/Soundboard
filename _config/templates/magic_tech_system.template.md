## 2. Sanderson's Law Analysis
- **First Law (Reader Clarity):** How transparent are the operational rules to the reader?
- **Second Law (Limitations & Absolute Negatives):**
  1. *Fuel Scarcity:* How difficult is it to obtain and replenish the power source?
  2. *Absolute Negatives:* The 3 non-negotiable things this power **can never do**.
  3. *Somatic & Psychological Cost:* Physical trauma, sensory blinding, madness, cellular decay.
  4. *Neutralization Window:* What substance or condition instantly deactivates the user?
- **Third Law (Societal Extrapolation):**
  - *Military Impact:* How has warfare adapted to counter or deploy this power?
  - *Economic Impact:* Who regulates the trade? What jobs were made obsolete?
  - *Legal & Criminal Impact:* How court trials, prisons, and theft syndicates exploit or suppress it.
