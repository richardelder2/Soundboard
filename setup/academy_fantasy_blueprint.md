# Magic Academy & Deadly Trials Fantasy Questionnaire

## STAGE 1: THE BRUTAL INSTITUTION & SENSORY STAKES
1. Welcome the author to the Academy War College. Ask if they are ready to construct a lethal, high-velocity fantasy universe where students must bond with apex beasts or die trying.
2. "What is the Academy, its geographic aerie, and its grim institutional purpose? (e.g., a cliffside war college training dragon-riders for a dying border, an underground necromantic conservatory harvesting void energy, an aerial fortress training storm-callers)?"
3. "What are the raw, sensory indicators of mortal danger here? Describe the smell of sulfur and wet scales, the sheer vertical drops from training parapets, the blood on stone sparring rings, and the cold discipline of uniform buckles."
4. "What is the **Deadly Selection Logic** on page one? How does the school demonstrate in Chapter 1 that selection is lethal and failure means death?"

## STAGE 2: THE TROPE STACK & THE BONDED BEAST
5. "Let's lock in your Trope Stack: **[dynamic] + [situation] + [flavor]**
   - Flagship Stack: *Magic Academy + Bonded Beast + Enemies-to-Lovers*
   - Dark Military Variant: *Rival Squads + Forbidden Magic + Touch Her and Die*
   - Survival Variant: *Outcast Bloodline + Apex Predator Bond + Underground Rebellion*
   What combination fuels your fire?"
6. "Introduce the **Bonded Beasts** (dragons, shadow-gryphons, abyssal drakes, chimeras):
   - What species are they, and how do they choose their riders/partners?
   - What does the mental and physical bond feel like?
   - What does the bond cost the human, and what agony happens if the bond is severed?"
7. "The Beast as a Character: What is the distinct personality, vocal tone, and behavioral attitude of our protagonist's beast? (e.g., arrogant, sarcastic, fiercely protective, bloodthirsty, aloof)?"

## STAGE 3: THE EXAM LADDER & INSTITUTIONAL ROT
8. "The **Exam Ladder (The Act Structure)**: Name the 3 distinct lethal trials the students must survive in Book 1:
   - Trial 1 (Tests cunning and agility over raw power):
   - Trial 2 (The mid-year Gauntlet—costs a close friend or ally's beast):
   - Trial 3 (The War Simulation / Climax trial where survival means passing together):"
9. "What are the hard rules and physical costs of the **Magic System**? (Remember: the rule that decides the climax must be stated and demonstrated through failure early on)."
10. "The **Institutional Rot**: The Academy leadership is lying to the cadets. What is the deep, dark secret of the war outside? (e.g., the enemy is fighting to stop an extinction event the Academy caused, the beasts are being enslaved by forbidden wards, the graduates are being purged)? When do the leads catch their first glimpse of this truth?"

## STAGE 4: THE UNDERDOG & THE ENEMY WINGLEADER
11. "Who is our Protagonist? Why should she statistically never survive the first week? (Smallest stature, wrong pedigree, late awakening, physical limitation)? What is her fierce, brilliant hidden edge?"
12. "Who is the **Enemy Love Interest** (The Wingleader / Rival House Heir / Commandant's Lethal Son)?
    - Why is their hatred structural and ideological (e.g., her family executed his brother, his squad leader ordered her conscription)?
    - In what exact scene does he fall first while she still wants to put a blade through his chest?"
13. "What is the **Squad Dynamic**? Who is the fiercely loyal best friend, the cocky rival who eventually becomes a brother-in-arms, and the snobbish antagonist within their barracks?"

## STAGE 5: THE MONEY SCENES & THE ACT 3 CRACK
14. "BookTok / Screenshot Money Scenes: Let's engineer the iconic visual set-pieces:
    - **The Bonding Scene**: The moment the most dangerous, feared beast bypasses the arrogant elites and chooses our battered underdog:
    - **The Sparring Mat Collision**: Hand-to-hand combat where hatred and raw physical attraction blur:
    - **The 'Touch Her and Die' Moment**: When an external bully threatens the heroine, and the hero and her beast both bare their teeth:"
15. "At 78%, the Act 3 Betrayal lands. What secret order from the leadership does the hero execute, or what truth about the heroine's bloodline detonates their fragile alliance?"
16. "How does the final trial collide with the external war? How do the beasts turn the tide, and what cliffhanger or darker truth propels the reader directly into Book 2?"

## STAGE 6: THE SHAPE OF THE TELLING
These answers set the structural authenticity dials in `_config/narrative_authenticity.md` and feed Stage 02's `structure_plan.md`.
17. "What POV structure gives maximum impact? (Deep, visceral first-person present focused on the heroine's survival and beast-telepathy, with an optional enemy POV epilogue)?"
18. "How will you maintain the Lore-Debt Ledger? (Keep track of sealed archives, ancient prophecies, and forbidden spells planted in Book 1 that will pay off in Books 2 and 3)."
19. "How will the prose balance fast-paced tactical flight/combat choreography with rich emotional angst and banter?"

## STAGE 7: SYNTHESIS
20. Review all answers. Synthesize them into an epic, blockbuster romantasy / academy fantasy narrative concept that commands instant attention. Present this back to the author.
21. Once approved, craft a 12-word pitch and a breathless series hook.
