## 2. BOOK BEAT SHEET — chapter skeleton (~70k words, ~28 chapters of 2,500 avg)

**ACT 1 — Arrival & Inheritance (ch. 1–7, 0–25%)**
- Ch. 1: MC arrives/inherits. **The shop rendered in full sensory detail** (obligatory: the shop is a character). The cat evaluates them. Verdict: pending.
- Ch. 2: The state of things — the shop's decline, the village's watchful welcome, one social misstep with a local institution.
- Ch. 3: **The discovery** — grandmother's recipe book / letters / the locked drawer. First hint the recipes/charms are more than they seem.
- Ch. 4: First attempt at the craft/magic — imperfect, revealing the RULES on page (magic rules stated before they ever resolve anything).
- Ch. 5: Shared-meal scene #1 (obligatory, one per act). A villager helped by a small kindness = found-family member #1 recruited.
- Ch. 6: The threat arrives, bureaucratic-scale: [inspection notice / developer's offer / the lease clause]. Deadline: the festival.
- Ch. 7: Act turn — MC commits to save the shop THE VILLAGE'S WAY, not the city way. The gatekeeper elder remains unconvinced.

**ACT 2A — Craft & Community (ch. 8–14, 25–50%)**
- Ch. 8–9: Apprenticeship chapters: learning the craft properly; the magic's COST paid on page for the first time.
- Ch. 10: Market-day set-piece; stall rivalry (gentle); found-family member #2 recruited via kindness.
- Ch. 11: The reclusive neighbor breached — persistent kindness beats; their knowledge unlocks a recipe/charm.
- Ch. 12: A charm/recipe helps a villager visibly — word spreads; the shop stirs to life. The cat's approval: partial.
- Ch. 13: The village secret, layer one — the discovery's letters connect the shop to [the founding bargain/the ley line].
- Ch. 14: **MIDPOINT:** the first small triumph (a successful tasting/reopening trial) AND the threat's counterstroke lands the same evening [the inspector's report / the rival bid accepted pending appeal]. Stakes clarified, still gentle.

**ACT 2B — The Deepening (ch. 15–21, 50–75%)**
- Ch. 15–16: The whole found family works the plan; shared-meal scene #2; romance subplot warms if present.
- Ch. 17: The magic misfires (the cat's disaster chapter) — comedic surface, revealing the ONE limit that will matter at the climax.
- Ch. 18: The gatekeeper elder's history revealed — WHY they guard the old ways; the MC finally listens instead of solving.
- Ch. 19: Layer two of the village secret; the MC's wound and the shop's history rhyme (the book's quiet emotional thesis).
- Ch. 20: Preparation montage for the festival; the village visibly assembling behind the shop.
- Ch. 21: The setback — the threat's final form [the hearing moved to festival day / the funds short by exactly ___]. A dark-ISH moment: the MC doubts they belong. Kept to one chapter; comfort contract holds.

**ACT 3 — Festival & Restoration (ch. 22–28, 75–100%)**
- Ch. 22–23: The village answers — each found-family member and even the stall rival contributes their piece (the community-assembles payoff, the genre's money scene).
- Ch. 24: The gatekeeper's public endorsement — the emotional climax (their approval was the real quest).
- Ch. 25–26: **Festival climax:** the craft/magic performed at its best, within its rules, its cost willingly paid; the bureaucratic antagonist defeated by [paperwork/community/conversion] — never humiliated, occasionally converted.
- Ch. 27: Restoration inventory: the shop reopened-transformed, the MC's wound eased in parallel, romance subplot resolved warmly, THE CAT'S FULL APPROVAL granted (the series' running final-beat).
- Ch. 28: Epilogue at the season's turn: shared-meal scene #3 with the whole family; next book's lead and shop glimpsed [the widower lingering by the shuttered bookbindery]. Recipe/charm appendix follows.

**Back matter:** the book's recipe cards PDF (newsletter magnet — this genre's best capture asset) → subscription box CTA → series page.

## 3. COZY FANTASY VARIANT (Burnt-Out Professional + Suspicious Village + Craft Mastery, 65–80k)
Deltas:
- Ch. 1: the breaking-point cold open in the OLD life (battle, boardroom, adventuring party) — ONE chapter maximum; readers came for the after.
- The MC's fearsome competence transfers charmingly (the mercenary's knife-work is excellent pastry lamination) — one transfer gag per act.
- The suspicious-village arc replaces inheritance: belonging earned by service; the gatekeeper thaws LAST.
- Mandatory beat at 70–80%: **the old life knocks** — the summons/offer/former comrade arrives; the refusal scene explicitly affirms "small life, done well."
- Climax may include ONE flash of the old competence in the shop's defense — then voluntarily set down again (the genre's signature image: the sword used to slice bread).
