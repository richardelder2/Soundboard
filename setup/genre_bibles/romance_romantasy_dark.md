## 2. BOOK BEAT SHEET — chapter skeleton (~100k words, ~38 chapters of 2,600 avg)

**ACT 1 (ch. 1–9, 0–25%)**
- Ch. 1: MC in her world + the wound visible. Hook line on page 1.
- Ch. 2: COLLISION with LI — hostile, public, personal. (Their enmity's cause must be real, not a misunderstanding.)
- Ch. 3: The proximity trap springs [bargain struck/marriage ordered/mission assigned]. Terms stated ON PAGE.
- Ch. 4–5: Resistance. Each tests the trap's walls and fails. First glimpse of his competence.
- Ch. 6–7: External threat's first strike. Forced to cooperate ONCE.
- Ch. 8: **Moment of unwilling respect** (obligatory scene #1).
- Ch. 9: Act turn — the trap deepens; no way back. *[HIS POV or tell: the reader learns he's already falling — he falls first is a STRUCTURAL choice, seed it here.]*

**ACT 2A (ch. 10–19, 25–50%)**
- Ch. 10–12: Reluctant teamwork. Banter sharpens to flirtation. One-bed / shared-quarters beat delivered on page (obligatory #2).
- Ch. 13: First vulnerability confession (his or hers — the other witnesses something unguarded).
- Ch. 14–15: Almost-kiss interrupted (obligatory #3). External plot clue.
- Ch. 16–17: He protects her AGAINST HIS OWN SIDE (obligatory #4 — the trope's signature beat).
- Ch. 18–19: **MIDPOINT: the tension snaps** — kiss or more, per heat level. Immediately complicated: [the bond/bargain/war makes it dangerous].

**ACT 2B (ch. 20–28, 50–75%)**
- Ch. 20–22: Stolen-moments phase. Intimacy escalates on your heat ladder. External stakes rise in parallel.
- Ch. 23: Her secret or his secret surfaces partially. (Hidden-identity reveals due by 60–70% — do not hold past it.)
- Ch. 24–26: The couple functions as a unit publicly — and their sides notice. The cost is named.
- Ch. 27–28: False victory against the external threat. One unguarded happy scene (readers screenshot this one — write it well).

**ACT 3 (ch. 29–38, 75–100%)**
- Ch. 29–30: **DARK MOMENT (75–80%):** betrayal or forced choice rooted in the ORIGINAL enmity — the wound from ch. 2 detonates. Separation.
- Ch. 31–33: Both leads act alone; each fails without the other. His grovel begins (proportional to his debt).
- Ch. 34–35: Grand gesture — he spends [power/position/safety] for her, publicly (obligatory #5).
- Ch. 36–37: External climax; the magic-system rule from the bible decides it; they win TOGETHER.
- Ch. 38: Resolution + HEA/HFN. **Epilogue:** domestic proof scene (obligatory #6). Book 2 hook in final lines if series-continuous.

**Back matter:** bonus-epilogue link (email capture) → series page → special edition CTA.

## 3. DARK ROMANCE VARIANT (Fated Mates + Morally Gray + Touch Her and Die, duet structure, 60–70k/book)

Deltas from the skeleton above:
- Ch. 1–2: The claiming/taking happens in Act 1 (dark romance front-loads the trap). Content warnings in front matter — a shelf-signal, not an apology.
- The bond manifests physically by ch. 3; **she RESISTS the bond visibly until midpoint** (choice must matter).
- Replace "moment of unwilling respect" with the **"why him" scene by 45–50%:** devotion proven through sacrifice/protection.
- Power balance ledger (unique tool): track leverage per chapter — she must gain real power over him by midpoint and near-parity before intimacy fully lands. If the ledger doesn't move, the book fails its readers.
- His darkness is never cured — it's AIMED. Climax = his violence spent entirely in her defense (touch-her-and-die payoff), her agency deciding the outcome.
- Duet split: Book 1 ends at maximum rupture (the bond/deal at breaking point). Book 2 = grovel, war, equalized HEA.
