# SERIES BIBLE & TEMPLATE: Fantasy / Sci-Fi / Horror
*Three templates: (A) **Magic Academy + Bonded Beast + Enemies-to-Lovers** (the Fourth Wing lane — your romantasy-crossover tentpole), (B) **Progression + Military SF + Underdog Awakening** (the KU velocity engine), (C) **Folk Horror + Missing-Person Spine + Dual Timeline** (commercial horror with thriller pacing).*

# TEMPLATE A: ACADEMY FANTASY (trilogy+, 110–120k/book)

## A1. SERIES BIBLE (fill-in)

- **The bonded beasts:** species [___] · how bonding chooses [___] · what the bond costs [___] · what severance does [___] · beast personalities are CHARACTERS [her beast's temperament ___]
- **Magic/power system:** what it does [___] · its cost [___] · the ONE rule that decides each book's climax [bk1 ___ / bk2 ___ / bk3 ___] (Sanderson's law: rules stated before they resolve anything)
- **MC:** why she shouldn't survive selection [smallest/late/wrong bloodline ___] · her hidden edge [___] · her wound [___] 
- **The LI (enemies-to-lovers):** his position [wingleader/rival house/the commandant's son ___] · why their enmity is STRUCTURAL [her family/his orders ___] · when he falls first [scene: ___]
- **The exam ladder (act structure, per book):** [Trial 1 ___ → Trial 2 ___ → Trial 3/Gauntlet ___] — each trial must test a DIFFERENT capacity and cost something real
- **The war outside:** what the academy feeds graduates into [___] · the lie the war is built on (the series' spine-twist) [___]
- **Lore-debt ledger (start it NOW):** every prophecy/sealed door/named-but-unseen thing → planted where → due when

## A2. BOOK 1 BEAT SHEET (~42 chapters of 2,700)

**ACT 1 (ch. 1–10):** Ch. 1 conscription/arrival — the mortal stakes of selection ON PAGE in the first chapter (someone doesn't make it). Ch. 2–3 the institution's rules + the enemy-LI collision (he expects her to die; say it aloud). Ch. 4–5 first classes; magic rules established through her failure. Ch. 6–7 the bonding trial approach; beast lore; a rival subplot opens. Ch. 8–9 **THE BONDING** — the beast chooses HER, inexplicably (the book's first money scene; write it for screenshots). Ch. 10 act turn: bonded, targeted, and noticed by the rot.

**ACT 2A (ch. 11–21):** Training-with-beast escalation; the LI forced to train her (proximity engine engaged); Trial 1 (ch. 14–15) — she survives on cleverness, not power; midnight-library/forbidden-archive beat (the rot's first document); the unwilling-respect scene; Trial 2 (ch. 19–20) — costs a friend or the friend's beast (the reader learns trials MEAN it); **midpoint ch. 21:** the almost-kiss collides with a rot-revelation — the institution lied about [___].

**ACT 2B (ch. 22–32):** Stolen training sessions become stolen moments; his secret orders revealed to READER (dramatic irony engine); the war touches the academy directly [attack/refugees/a graduate's body]; her hidden edge manifests publicly — now the leadership wants her; the kiss (ch. 28); Trial 3 prep under sabotage; ch. 31–32 false victory — Trial 3/Gauntlet passed TOGETHER, bond and romance at full strength for one golden chapter.

**ACT 3 (ch. 33–42):** The betrayal (his orders execute, or her bloodline's truth detonates) at 78%; separation + the rot's face revealed; the external climax fuses war + trials + the magic rule from the bible; the beasts turn the battle (payoff of every beast-personality beat); the war-lie cracks open in the final chapters — resolved enough to satisfy, open enough to compel; final line = the series promise restated darker. **Epilogue:** the enemy POV (the map is bigger than the school).

# TEMPLATE B: PROGRESSION / MILITARY SF (open series, 100–140k/book, 60–90 day cycle)

## B1. SERIES BIBLE (fill-in)

- **The System:** how power is measured [ranks/levels/gates ___] · advancement mechanics [___] · what the reader can COUNT [visible stat/rank markers every ___ chapters] · the system's hidden layer (his unique skill) [___]
- **MC:** ranked last because [___] · the awakening that changes it [the skill no one else can see ___] · why he hides it initially [___]
- **The war:** enemy [___] · humanity's real position [losing, lied about] · **the corporate/command lie** [___] (near-obligatory beat)
- **The unit (found family):** 4–6 members [the sergeant ___, the rival-turned-brother ___, the tech ___] — one promotion/loss per book
- **Power escalation ledger (the series' spine — maintain religiously):** rank at book start/end · new abilities · known ceilings · NO forgotten powers, NO regression without payoff
- **Release math check:** at 60–90 day cycles this template assumes outline-to-draft speed; if a book slips past 120 days, simplify the plot, never the progression beats.

## B2. BOOK BEAT SHEET (rhythm-based — this genre is metronomic; ~50 chapters)

- **Every 4–6 chapters: a measurable advancement** (rank-up, skill unlock, breakthrough). This is the product; everything else is connective tissue.
- **Ch. 1–3:** Bottom of the ladder, unfairly [the cheat/the test rigged/the late awakening]. The unfairness must be VISCERAL — reader indignation is the fuel.
- **Ch. 4–8:** The awakening; the hidden skill's rules demonstrated small; first quiet win over a sneering superior (the genre's dopamine primer).
- **Ch. 9–20 (Act: Boot/Gauntlet):** training arcs, unit bonding, Advancement #1 and #2; the rival established; first real engagement — tactical clarity mandatory (readers diagram these); a loss that sticks.
- **Ch. 21–26 (Midpoint arc):** the tournament/selection/first deployment set-piece — his hidden skill half-exposed; Advancement #3; the command lie glimpsed in a sealed order.
- **Ch. 27–40 (Act: The Front):** escalating engagements, each showcasing a NEW application of his skill (novel use > raw power — the genre's craft bar); unit casualties with names; the rival converts to brother-in-arms; Advancement #4; the lie's edge cuts the unit directly.
- **Ch. 41–50 (Climax arc):** the impossible engagement; his skill's TRUE nature revealed at maximum cost; Advancement #5 (the book's rank-up crescendo — end every book one full tier higher than it began); the war's next layer revealed in the final chapter — **never end a progression book on a resolved note; end on the next mountain.**
- **Back matter:** rank-sheet appendix (readers love the ledger) → next book preorder (which should already exist — cycle discipline).

# TEMPLATE C: FOLK HORROR (standalone, 80–90k, branded pen name)

## C1. BOOK BIBLE (fill-in)

- **The community:** place [___] · its beauty (rendered genuinely seductive) [___] · **the old practice** [___] · what it actually maintains/appeases [___] · **the community BELIEVES** (they are sincere, never cynical) [___]
- **The rite calendar:** the festival the plot bends toward [___, date] — the book's ticking clock
- **The missing person (the thriller spine):** who vanished [___] · their connection to the outsider-MC [sister/son/the previous researcher] · what the community says happened [___] · what did [___]
- **The outsider MC:** why they come [the search ___] · their rationality (what they'll rationalize longest) [___]
- **Dual timeline:** NOW = the search; THEN = [the missing person's final weeks / the practice's origin year] · convergence point [the rite, ch. ___]
- **The entity/force ruleset:** what it can do [___] · its escalation logic [___] · what it's owed [___] · the letter-of-the-deal law [___]
- **The ending (choose deliberately):** [escape at price / the old way wins / the outsider converts] — horror may end dark, but it must land AUTHORED.
- **The screenshot image:** the ONE image reviews will describe [___] — engineer it, place it at ~60%.

## C2. BEAT SHEET (~40 chapters of 2,200, timelines alternating 2:1 now:then)

**ACT 1 (ch. 1–10):** NOW: arrival — pastoral beauty, exceptional hospitality, the missing person's cold trail; one wrongness at the edge of frame (a symbol repeated, a door always freshly painted). THEN: the missing person arrives in the same shots, warm-toned — the dread is the rhyme. Ch. 9–10: the community's kindly evasions harden by one degree; the festival is announced; the MC is warmly, insistently invited.

**ACT 2A (ch. 11–20):** The investigation-vs-hospitality dance: every question answered pleasantly and wrongly; church records with a page gone; the one villager who almost speaks (and is gently collected by neighbors mid-sentence — a signature beat). THEN-timeline: the missing person's seduction by belonging (they were HAPPY here — the horror's cruelest note). **Midpoint ch. 20:** the MC witnesses a fragment of the practice — explicable, almost; their rationality holds by a thread the reader has already lost.

**ACT 2B (ch. 21–30):** Isolation mechanics: phone/car/road failures, all plausible; the THEN timeline reaches its own festival — and cuts away at the threshold; the proof-scene (~60%): **the screenshot image** — the MC finds [the wall of photographs / the field of ___ ] and rationality ends; escape attempt #1 fails with terrifying politeness ("the bus doesn't run festival week"); the entity's ruleset assembles from folklore fragments — what it's owed, and that the missing person was [volunteer/chosen/payment].

**ACT 3 (ch. 31–40):** The festival arrives; timelines converge — the THEN threshold finally crossed on page, intercut with NOW; the truth of the missing person [alive-and-changed / the harvest's heart / the thirteenth dancer]; the MC's choice under the entity's letter-of-the-deal law [take their place / take them and owe / burn it and doom the valley]; the price PAID on page; denouement brief and wrong-lit — the world outside resumed, one detail carried back that shouldn't be. Final page: the practice continues / the debt travels — per your chosen ending.

**Back matter:** the "field notes/case file" bonus PDF (the merch-synergy asset from Playbook 05) → pen-name backlist → October campaign email capture.

## SHARED TRACKERS (all three templates)
- **Lore-debt ledger** (fantasy/SF): promise → planted → due. Audit at every book's outline stage; unpaid debt at series end poisons backlist ads.
- **Power/rank escalation ledger** (progression): the series' single most-audited document — readers keep their own; yours must be better.
- **Rules-consistency audit** (all three): magic/system/entity rules stated on page BEFORE any climax uses them; violations are the #1 review-killer in this cluster.
- **Screenshot inventory:** 2 engineered images per book (the bonding, the wall of photographs, the rank-up crescendo) — your BookTok/ads creative pipeline.
