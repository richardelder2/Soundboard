# Progression Fantasy & Military Sci-Fi Questionnaire

## STAGE 1: THE SYSTEM & SENSORY COMBAT GRIT
1. Welcome the author to the Progression War Room. Ask if they are ready to construct a metronomic, numbers-go-up engine of tactical survival, squad brotherhood, and power escalation.
2. "What is the universe and battlefield arena? (e.g., a trench-warfare planetary siege against biomechanical swarms, an orbital gate system where humans fight celestial entities, an interstellar drop-trooper legion exploring hostile alien ruins)?"
3. "What are the tactile sensory details of the combat line? Describe the smell of spent plasma cells and cordite, the hiss of hydraulic exo-suits, the HUD flickering with bio-vitals in red, and the muddy boots in the trenches."
4. "What is the **Progression System**? How is power objectively measured in this world? (e.g., Core Tiers, Gate Ranks F-through-S, Nanite Density percentages, Cultivation Realms, LitRPG stat interfaces)?"

## STAGE 2: THE TROPE STACK & THE HIDDEN SKILL
5. "Let's lock in your Trope Stack: **[dynamic] + [situation] + [flavor]**
   - Flagship Stack: *Progression + Military SF + Underdog Awakening*
   - LitRPG Variant: *Glitched Class + Survival Arena + Crafting Integration*
   - Cybernetic/Cultivation Variant: *Scrapper Upgrade + Megacorp War + Found Family Squad*
   What loop makes your gamer brain sing?"
6. "Why does our protagonist start at the very bottom of the ladder? How is the system or recruitment test rigged against them in Chapter 1 to create visceral, boiling reader indignation?"
7. "What is the **Awakening & The Hidden Skill**? When everyone else gets standard combat proficiencies, what overlooked, broken, or unique ability unlocks for our lead? Why must they conceal its full power initially?"

## STAGE 3: THE SYSTEM RULES & POWER ESCALATION LEDGER
8. "What are the hard, quantifiable rules of advancement? What must a fighter consume, endure, or accomplish to rank up (e.g., absorbing core crystals, surviving near-death threshold pressure, decrypting ancient combat codices)?"
9. "The **Power Escalation Ledger**: In this genre, progression is metronomic (advancements occur every 4–6 chapters). Map out the 5 measurable tier advancements in Book 1:
   - Advancement 1 (Ch. 5–8): Quiet initial breakthrough over an arrogant superior:
   - Advancement 2 (Ch. 12–15): The first tactical combat test in the field:
   - Advancement 3 (Ch. 22–25): The mid-point tournament or siege set-piece:
   - Advancement 4 (Ch. 32–36): Novel application of their hidden skill under extreme crisis:
   - Advancement 5 (Ch. 45–48): The climactic rank-up crescendo that carries them to the next tier:"
10. "Tactical Rule: In elite progression craft, **novel use of existing power outclasses raw brute force**. Give an example of how the protagonist uses their power with cunning geometry, chemistry, or trickery."

## STAGE 4: THE UNIT (FOUND FAMILY) & THE RIVAL
11. "Who is our Underdog Protagonist? What is their relentless internal drive, and what physical or social deficit forced them to develop superior tactical grit?"
12. "Introduce the **Found Family Combat Unit** (4–6 distinct archetypes):
    - The grizzled, cigar-chomping veteran sergeant who spots the kid's potential:
    - The sneering elite rival who slowly converts into a ride-or-die battle brother:
    - The eccentric technician / medic whose gadgets keep them barely alive:
    - The rookie whose tragic loss will raise the stakes:"
13. "Who is the **Command / Corporate Lie**? High command is lying about the war. What dirty secret are soldiers being fed into the meat grinder to protect?"

## STAGE 5: THE SIEGE & THE CLIMACTIC CRESCENDO
14. "What is the Midpoint Engagement? (e.g., a doomed recon drop behind enemy lines, holding a defensive bunker against overwhelming waves, the tournament showcase where their skill is half-revealed)?"
15. "The Climax Arc: Describe the impossible final engagement where the unit is cut off and the protagonist must unleash their full, hidden potential at massive personal cost."
16. "The Next Mountain: Progression novels NEVER end on a fully resolved, resting note. How does the final chapter reveal the NEXT threat tier, rank ladder, or galaxy-wide stakes?"

## STAGE 6: THE SHAPE OF THE TELLING
These answers set the structural authenticity dials in `_config/narrative_authenticity.md` and feed Stage 02's `structure_plan.md`.
17. "How should the tactical action be paced? (Progression readers demand crisp geometric choreography: cover distances, ammunition counts, cooldown times, and spatial awareness must remain crystal clear)."
18. "How will you format the Status Sheet / HUD updates? (Inline system notifications, end-of-chapter ledger summaries, or diegetic HUD voice)?"
19. "What dark humor, military gallows banter, and canteen rituals keep the tone authentic between brutal firefights?"

## STAGE 7: SYNTHESIS
20. Review all answers. Synthesize them into an addictive, high-octane progression military fantasy/sci-fi concept summary. Present this back to the author.
21. Once approved, craft a 12-word pitch and a punchy, numbers-driven blurb hook.
