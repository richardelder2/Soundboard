# Romantasy & Dark Romance Questionnaire

## STAGE 1: THE ATMOSPHERE & SENSUAL TENSION
1. Welcome the author to the Romantasy & Dark Romance salon. Ask if they are ready to engineer a high-stakes collision of power, magic, and intoxicating romantic tension.
2. "What is the sensory world of this romance? What does this realm look, smell, and feel like? (e.g., glittering winter courts with black ice and velvet, ash-strewn volcanic fortresses with blood wine and iron chains, decaying gothic estates shrouded in bioluminescent mist?)"
3. "What are the tactile markers of intimacy and danger between the leads? (e.g., the scrape of leather armor, the cold press of a dagger to the throat, the scent of crushed cedar and ozone when power flares?)"
4. "What is the Heat Level and spice profile for this story? Choose your lane and hold it: Closed Door (sweet tension), Warm (sensual fade-to-black), or Explicit / Scorching (on-page high heat with dark romance tropes)?"

## STAGE 2: THE TROPE STACK & THE PROXIMITY ENGINE
5. "Let's lock in your Trope Stack: **[dynamic] + [situation] + [flavor]**
   - Romantasy Classic: *Enemies to Lovers + Forced Proximity + He Falls First*
   - Dark Romance Variant: *Fated Mates + Morally Gray Antihero + Touch Her and Die*
   - Custom Stack: What combination makes your pulse race?"
6. "What is the **Proximity Engine**? What inescapable circumstance forces these two lethal enemies to share the same small space, bed, or war council for 60%+ of the novel? (e.g., political marriage alliance, bonded by blood-oath, sharing a single horse / prison cell, chained together across hostile territory?)"
7. "Why is leaving completely impossible for BOTH of them? What mortal consequence or magical backlash triggers if either walks away?"

## STAGE 3: THE MAGIC COST & ARENA CONFLICT
8. "What is the external war or political feud that divides their factions? Why is this conflict intensely personal to both leads, making their attraction feel like an act of treason?"
9. "What are the rules of the magic or supernatural power system? (Remember Sanderson's First Law: magic's ability to solve problems is directly proportional to how well the reader understands its costs and limits)."
10. "What is the ONE specific magic rule or metaphysical oath that will decide the climax of the book?"

## STAGE 4: THE LEADS & THE EMOTIONAL WOUNDS
11. "Who is our Heroine / Protagonist?
    - What is her outward weapon or lethal competence?
    - What is the internal wound and the Lie she believes about herself?
    - What hidden vulnerability or beauty does she alone see inside the monster/enemy?"
12. "Who is the Love Interest (The Morally Gray Commander / Shadow Prince / Brutal Protector)?
    - What is his position of power, and why does his society fear him?
    - Why does **HE FALL FIRST**? Describe the exact private moment where he realizes she owns him, even while she still despises him."
13. "What is the Love Interest's **Grovel Debt**? What terrible choice or past act of his did real damage to the heroine that he must spend blood and pride atoning for before the final union?"

## STAGE 5: THE ESCALATION & THE BETRAYAL
14. "The Romance Escalation Ladder: Trace the progression of their fire:
    - The First Spark (knife-to-throat verbal spar with undeniable electricity):
    - The 'Only One Bed / Shared Warmth' moment:
    - The First Kiss / First Surrender:
    - The 'Who Did This to You / Touch Her and Die' set-piece:"
15. "At 75–80%, the Act 3 Betrayal lands. What secret order, ancient curse, or hidden allegiance detonates, forcing them apart just as they had finally surrendered to their feelings?"
16. "How does the climax force both leads to fight side-by-side at the cost of their own safety? What is the final declaration or sacrifice that cements their earned Happily Ever After (or Happy For Now)?"

## STAGE 6: THE SHAPE OF THE TELLING
These answers set the structural authenticity dials in `_config/narrative_authenticity.md` and feed Stage 02's `structure_plan.md`.
17. "What POV approach best serves this chemistry? (Dual POV alternating chapters—giving the reader delicious dramatic irony—or single first-person deep POV focused on the heroine's intense subjective experience?)"
18. "How will you handle the pacing of the tension? (Authentic romantasy lets the hostility simmer with genuine ideological friction before the romantic shift—avoiding instant love)."
19. "What loyal companion, war-beast, or comedic second-in-command provides respite and banter between the heavy romantic set-pieces?"

## STAGE 7: SYNTHESIS
20. Review all answers. Synthesize them into an electrifying, emotionally charged narrative concept summary. Present this back to the author and ask: "Does this capture the heat, the danger, and the emotional resonance you want?"
21. Once approved, craft a 12-word pitch designed to blow up BookTok / Bookstagram and a killer series hook.
