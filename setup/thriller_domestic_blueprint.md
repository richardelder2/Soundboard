# Domestic Thriller & Psychological Suspense Questionnaire

## STAGE 1: THE ENVIABLE SURFACE & SENSORY CHILL
1. Welcome the author to the Domestic Thriller brainstorming room. Ask if they are ready to construct a psychological puzzle box where nothing in the house is safe.
2. "Describe the enviable surface life that readers will covet for the opening three chapters. What is the house, the marriage, the social status, or the career that looks flawless from the sidewalk?"
3. "What is the primary sensory atmosphere of this domestic space? Contrast the warm, curated luxury with the cold, uneasy undertones (e.g., floor-to-ceiling glass walls that feel like a terrarium, silent smart locks clicking into place, the low hum of HVAC in an empty four-bedroom house)."
4. "What is the tech vector or modern convenience that was supposed to make life easier, but will become the cage? (e.g., a whole-home automated security system, a shared family tracking app, an AI nursery monitor, a deepfake harassment campaign?)"

## STAGE 2: THE GASLIGHT ENGINE & THE TROPE STACK
5. "Let's dial in the core trope stack: **[dynamic] + [situation] + [flavor]** (e.g., *Perfect-Life Lie + Gaslight Engine + Techno-Paranoia*, or *Unreliable Memory + Toxic Sister-in-Law + Gated Suburb*). What dynamic excites you most?"
6. "What is 'Wrongness #1'—the first small, deniable discrepancy in the protagonist's life? (e.g., an app login at 3:14 AM from inside the house, an expensive package ordered that she has no memory of buying, a prescription bottle moved to a different drawer) How does she rationalize it away at first?"
7. "What is the 'evidence-both-ways' hook? Name an event where external observers see proof that the protagonist is unravelling, while the protagonist sees terrifying proof that she is being targeted."

## STAGE 3: THE ARCHITECT & THE FAIR-PLAY CLUES
8. "Who is the Architect behind this psychological torture, and what is their cold, meticulous motive? (Remember: the villain wrote their timeline forward; the reader discovers it backward.)"

10. "What is the analog escape route? When all digital access is revoked and the protagonist is locked out of her accounts, what physical, analog tool or unsophisticated method lets her fight back?"

## STAGE 4: THE PROTAGONIST & THE SUSPECT WEB
11. "Who is our protagonist? What is her outward competence (profession or social role), and what is the private guilt or past trauma that makes her vulnerable to self-doubt?"
12. "Who is the primary Confidant/Ally she turns to when the walls close in—and what subtle flaw or inconsistency will make the reader question this person's true allegiance?"
13. "Who is the Institutional Antagonist? (e.g., the calm family doctor who prescribes heavier sedatives, the polite detective who treats her like a hysterical spouse, the partner whose concern feels suffocating?)"

## STAGE 5: THE MIDPOINT & THE 85% TWIST
14. "At the Midpoint (Chapter 23), the protagonist must acquire the 'Proof-Object'—one undeniable physical or digital artifact proving she isn't crazy. What is this object, and how does acquiring it alert the Architect that she knows?"
15. "What is the 85% Recontextualizing Twist? State it in a single punchy sentence (e.g., *The husband was the victim and the sister was the architect*, or *The house was never broken into because she never left the clinic*)."
16. "How does the climax turn the Architect's own system against them? In what way does the cage become the trap for the villain?"

## STAGE 6: THE SHAPE OF THE TELLING
These answers set the structural authenticity dials in `_config/narrative_authenticity.md` and feed Stage 02's `structure_plan.md`.
17. "What POV and timeline structure do you want? (e.g., single intimate first-person present, dual 'Now / Then' timeline counting down to a police interview, or dual POV between the target and the hidden architect?)"
18. "How adventurous should the narrative timeline be? Will you use an in media res flash-forward prologue with a chilling image that only makes sense 40 chapters later?"
19. "What messy, unresolved subplot would you like running underneath the main thriller spine? (e.g., an aging parent's dementia that mirrors the protagonist's fears of memory loss, or a petty neighborhood feud that provides noisy misdirection?)"

## STAGE 7: SYNTHESIS
20. Review all answers. Synthesize them into a taut, pitch-perfect domestic suspense narrative summary. Present this back to the author and ask: "How does this gaslight architecture feel? Are the planted clues fair, and does the twist hit with maximum psychological impact?"
21. Once approved, craft a 12-word pitch and a commercial logline ready for the book jacket.
