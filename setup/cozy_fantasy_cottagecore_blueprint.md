# Cozy Fantasy & Cottagecore Questionnaire

## STAGE 1: THE WARM HUG & CULINARY/CRAFT SENSORY COMFORTS
1. Welcome the author to the hearth of the Cozy Fantasy studio. Ask if they are ready to build a story that feels like wrapping up in a warm hand-knit blanket with a fresh pot of herbal tea.
2. "What is the village or sanctuary setting? Describe its gentle beauty and soul (e.g., a quiet valley of stone cottages surrounded by enchanted ancient blackberry briars, an alpine harbor where airships deliver spices and lavender, a hidden forest clearing with a moss-roofed tavern?)"
3. "What are the rich sensory details of daily life here? Describe the sounds of bread crust crackling, bubbling cider, rain tapping on stained glass, or the smell of dried chamomile and polished beeswax."
4. "The Cozy Pen-Name Promise: 'Nobody dies, the animal is fine, the shop is saved, and you'll want the tea.' What is the emotional sanctuary feeling you want readers to experience?"

## STAGE 2: THE TROPE STACK & THE HOMELY CRAFT
5. "Let's choose your Trope Stack: **[dynamic] + [situation] + [flavor]**
   - Flagship Stack: *Inherited Shop + Hedge Witch / Kitchen Magic + Shop Cat*
   - Burnt-Out Professional Variant: *Retired Barbarian / Sorcerer + Slow Food Craft + Found Family*
   - Wandering Variant: *Traveling Apothecary + Forest Spirits + Village Restoration*
   What flavor speaks to your soul?"
6. "What is the **Shop or Craft** at the center of this story? (e.g., a bespoke tea-blending salon, a magical bookbindery, a hearth-witch bakery, a charm-carver's workshop, a quiet tavern serving orcish pastries)?"
7. "What was the previous state of this shop, and what charming or mysterious legacy did the previous owner leave behind (e.g., handwritten recipe journals, a garden of talking herbs, a locked attic with friendly soot sprites)?"

## STAGE 3: GENTLE MAGIC & STAKES CEILINGS
8. "What are the rules of the **Gentle Magic** in this world?
   - What small, everyday enchantments exist? (e.g., bread that cures heartbreak, tea that grants peaceful sleep, candles that reveal lost memories)?
   - What is its homely cost or requirement (e.g., magic only works if baked with sincere goodwill, a memory given for a charm)?
   - What is its absolute limit?"
9. "The **Stakes Ceiling Check**: In cozy fantasy, the world is NOT in mortal danger. What is the worst realistic outcome the protagonist faces? (e.g., failing to pay an unexpected guild tax, failing an inspection by an overzealous bureaucrat, being unable to finish the festival orders in time)? If anyone could die, scale it back!"
10. "Who is the **Bureaucratic Recurring Antagonist**? (e.g., the pompous municipal tax inspector, the skeptical guild assessor, the stuffy neighbor who complains about chimney smoke) How will they be disarmed—often by kindness, good food, or community solidarity?"

## STAGE 4: THE CRAFTSPERSON, THE COMPANION & THE FOUND FAMILY
11. "Who is our Protagonist? What exhausted them in the wider world (the corporate grind, the bloody adventurer's life, an academic failure), and what quiet restoration are they seeking here?"
12. "Introduce **THE CAT** (or shop hedgehog, miniature dragon, opinionated raven):
    - What is their name and physical quirk?
    - How do they judge incoming customers?
    - What is their one harmless disaster per book?
    - (Contractual Guarantee: The pet survives 100% unharmed!)."
13. "Who joins the **Found Family** circle as the shop grows? Name 2 or 3 quirky village locals who wander into the shop and slowly become indispensable helpers and friends."

## STAGE 5: THE VILLAGE FESTIVAL & GENTLE RESTORATION
14. "What seasonal festival or communal milestone is approaching? (e.g., The Midsummer Hearth Market, The Autumn Apple Pressing, The Winter Solstice Lantern Walk)?"
15. "What is the gentle crisis that threatens the festival or the shop's grand opening—and how do the villagers rally to help our protagonist overcome it?"
16. "Is there a closed-door, sweet, slow-paced romance subplot? If so, who is the quiet, supportive local (e.g., the shy woodworker who builds the shelves, the local librarian who translates the recipes)?"

## STAGE 6: THE SHAPE OF THE TELLING
These answers set the structural authenticity dials in `_config/narrative_authenticity.md` and feed Stage 02's `structure_plan.md`.
17. "How should the narrative pace unfold? (Cozy craft thrives on a rhythmic, episodic flow: alternating between quiet baking/crafting sequences, warm customer encounters, and gradual community connection)."
18. "What village secret will be peeled back across books? (e.g., the ancient friendly spirit sleeping beneath the village mill, the origins of the festival)? How much is revealed in Book 1?"
19. "Would you like each chapter to open with an in-world element (e.g., an artisanal recipe, a tea-steeping guide, or an excerpt from an herbology journal)?"

## STAGE 7: SYNTHESIS
20. Review all answers. Synthesize them into an utterly comforting, warm-hearted cozy fantasy pitch and series blueprint. Present this back to the author.
21. Once approved, craft a cozy 12-word pitch and an inviting series tag.
